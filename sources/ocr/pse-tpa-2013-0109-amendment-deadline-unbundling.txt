LAUNCHING OF THE UNBUNDLING SYSTEM AND REVISED
DEADLINE FOR SUBMISSION OF AMENDMENT AND UNBUNDLING
Please be informed that the PSE Unbundling System will be launched on August 05, 2013. In
relation thereto, all aggregated orders done from August 05, 2013 onwards should be
unbundled using the said unbundling facility, no unbundling shall be done using the amendment
system.
The Securities and Exchange Commission directed the Exchange to amend the deadline for
unbundling of aggregated orders and other related rules. Quoted below is the said directive from
the Commission:
"RESOLVED, To DIRECT the Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE):

the unbundling of bundled accounts, whether there is a change in nationality flag
or not, should be made not later than 12:00 p.m. of T+l

affected. "
