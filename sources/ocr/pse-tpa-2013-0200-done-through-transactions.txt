APPROVED AMENDMENT TO THE REVISED TRADING RULES
REGARDING ACCOUNT CODES AND DONE THROUGH
TRANSACTIONS
Please be informed that the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) approved the
amendments to the Revised Trading Rules and its Implementing Guidelines governing
Account Codes and Done Through Transactions.
Relatively, all existing account codes for done through should be modified to Special Account
Institutional via iPSE System of the Exchange under the Client Code Generation module.
Modification done before 4:00 p.m. will be activated the following business day and any
modification done beyond 4:00 p.m. will be activated after two (2) business days.
Upon activation of the modified account code to Special Account Institutional, all existing
non-day orders under the old account code shall be automatically cancelled. TPs may re-enter
the affected orders under the Special Account Institutional.
Attached is a copy of the amended sections. Kindly ensure that all necessary modifications
have been effected before January 02, 2014.
For your information and guidance.
