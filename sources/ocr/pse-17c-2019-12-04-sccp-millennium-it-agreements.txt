Subject of the Disclosure
The Securities Clearing Corporation of the Philippines ("SCCP"), a wholly owned subsidiary of the Philippine Stock
Exchange, Inc. (PSE), entered into Software License and Maintenance Agreement and a Consultancy Services'
Agreement with Millennium IT Software (Private) Limited ("Millennium IT"), a subsidiary unit of the London Stock
Exchange Group.
Background/Description of the Disclosure
The SCCP entered into a Software License and Maintenance Agreement and a Consultancy Services' Agreement with
Millennium IT for the customization, use and maintenance of the Millennium Post Trade solutions and Millennium Risk
products, which will replace SCCP's current clearing and settlement, risk and collateral management systems.
The installation of this new clearing and settlement system will enable SCCP to improve and enhance the services it
provides to the market.
