COMMENCEMENT OF OPERATIONS OF
THE CAPITAL MARKETS INTEGRITY CORPORATION
March 15, 2012
Please be reminded that pursuant to the order of the Securities and Exchange
Commission (SEC) dated March 9, 2012, the Capital Markets Integrity Corporation
(CMIC) was authorized to commence operations and to discharge its functions as the
independent audit, surveillance and compliance unit of the Exchange effective March
12, 2012.
The CMIC, a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Exchange, was created to take over and
perform the functions of the Market Regulation Division (MRD) and Market Integrity
Board of the Exchange, with the end in view of reinforcing the confidence of the
investing public in the stock market through the adoption and independent enforcement,
implementation and interpretation of applicable rules, including the CMIC Rules,
guidelines and securities laws.
In view of the afore-mentioned SEC order, all audit, surveillance and compliance
functions of the MRD shall be fully and exclusively assumed by the CMIC effective
March 12, 2012.
