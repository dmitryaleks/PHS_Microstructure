This Consultation Paper is circulated to give concerned stakeholders an opportunity to submit
their comments, if any, on the proposed amendments to the PSE Rules on Exchange Traded Funds
("PSE ETF Rules").
Interested parties may submit their comments and requests for clarifications by e-mail to
ogc list@pse.com.ph until June 30, 2026.
IL Rationale for the Rule Review
To encourage a wider range of issuers, the Exchange is proposing significant amendments to the
current ETF Listing Rules. These revisions aim to lower the barriers to entry for potential issuers
while providing greater operational flexibility in fund management and corporate structuring.
The proposed amendments also align PSE's framework with the rules in other markets and SEC
Memorandum Circular No. 14, s. 2026 ("SEC Rules on Umbrella Fund"), which permits the
establishment of multi-portfolio investment companies with segregated sub-funds.
III. Proposed Amendments
The key amendments include the following:
I. Umbrella Structure: The current rules only contemplate single-fund listings while the
proposed rules allow "Umbrella ETFs" where multiple portfolios or sub-funds with
different investment objectives and strategies can be offered and listed under one ETF
issuer, dispensing with the need to incorporate and list a separate entity for each sub-
fund.
2. Expanded List of ETF Issuers: The proposed rules include Unit Investment Trust Funds
(UITFs) and other collective investment schemes as eligible ETF issuers, moving beyond
just open-end investment companies. Thus, under the proposed rules, the Exchange will
accept the listing of different types of securities such as fund units, in addition to shares
of an ETF Company.
3. Active Management: The proposed rules explicitly allow actively-managed ETFs in
addition to traditional passive index-tracking funds.
4. Reduced Capitalization: The minimum paid-up capital requirement for ETF issuers is
lowered from Php250 million to Php50 million.

5. Simplified Participation: The proposed rules likewise reduce the required number of
Authorized Participants (i.e., broker dealers that handle the creation and redemption of
ETF shares or units) to just one (1) Authorized Participant and remove the requirement
for one of the Authorized Participants to be the Market Maker.
6. Global Diversification: Lastly, the proposed rules allow ETFs to track underlying
securities listed in foreign exchanges or regulated OTC markets.

PART A
GENERAL PROVISIONS
Section 1. Scope - These Rules on Exchange Traded Funds ("Rules") are established to govern
the listing and trading of Exchange Traded Funds ("ETF") in the Philippine Stock Exchange.
Section 2. Introduction An Exchange Traded Fund or ETF is a collective investment scheme
listed in an Exchange that continuously issues and redeems units of participation or shares of
stock in Creation Unit in exchange for delivery of a basket of securities or assets representing an
index whose performance the ETF endeavors to track or outperform; provided that, the terms
and conditions relative to the issuance and redemption in creation unit shall be prescribed and
disclosed in its Registration Statement.
An ETF duly approved by the Commission to operate as a multi-portfolio investment company
or umbrella ETF may apply for the listing of shares, units, or securities issued by the ETF with
respect to one or more Portfolios, subject to these Rules, the SEC ETF Rules, and such
requirements as the Exchange may prescribe. For purposes of these Rules, unless the context
otherwise requires, references to "ETF shares or units" shall include shares, units, or securities
issued by the ETF with respect to a specific Portfolio.
A U'Portfolio" refers to a distinct sub-fund established within a multi-portfolio ETF or umbrella
ETF approved by the Commission, with its own investment objective, benchmark, investment
policy, assets, liabilities, financial records, NAV, NAVps or NAVpu, basket of securities or
assets, creation and redemption arrangements, and listed securities.
A "Multi-Portfolio ETF" or "Umbrella ETF" refers to an ETF approved by the Commission to
establish two or more distinct Portfolios within a single corporate entity, each of which may
have a distinct series of shares, units, or securities issued by the ETF and listed and traded on
the Exchange in accordance with these Rules.
Section 3. Supplemental Rules - These Rules shall be read in conjunction with the SEC ETF Rules,
the Securities Regulation Code and its implementing rules and regulations, the Investment
Company Act and other relevant laws, rules and regulations, as amended, all of which shall form
part of these Rules. All rules of the Exchange not inconsistent with these Rules shall apply to
ETFs.

Section 5. Listing of ETF Securities - The shares, units, or securities issued by an ETF shall be
listed on the Exchange's Main Board.
For purposes of listing on the Exchange, only the shares, units, or securities issued by the ETF
shall be listed and traded. The listing of such shares, units, or securities shall not be construed
as the listing of the ETF itself, nor shall it confer upon the ETF the status of a listed corporation
or trust under the Exchange's Listing Rules. The ETF shall, however, comply with the
continuing listing, disclosure, and other obligations expressly applicable to ETFs under these
Rules.
In the case of a Multi-Portfolio ETF, each series of shares, units, or securities issued by the ETF
with respect to a Portfolio approved for listing will likewise be listed and traded on the Main
Board as a separate listed security and under its own ticker symbol, subject to such naming,
trading, disclosure, and operational requirements as the Exchange may prescribe. The initial
listing requirements under the Listing Rules are applicable to ETFs, unless otherwise provided
herein or inconsistent with these Rules.

Section 10, Delisting of an ETF
a. In addition to the grounds for involuntary delisting under the Delisting Rules, the following
shall be grounds for the involuntary delisting of the shares, units, or securities issued by
an ETF, or the shares, units, or securities issued with respect to a specific Portfolio of a
Multi-Portf01i0 ETF:
a) Failure by the ETF or any Portfolio to comply with its continuing listing obligations,
including the failure to pay the applicable fees;
b) Continued breach of the tracking error threshold set by the ETF or Portfolio for a
period of at least one year. Tracking error as defined in the SEC ETF Rules Section
5.21 is the standard deviation of the difference in relative returns between the ETF or
Portfolio and its underlying index or benchmark;
c) Revocation of the registration of the ETF and/or its shares, units, Portfolio securities,
or any Portfolio;
d) Such other grounds as may be determined by the Exchange, where delisting of the
ETF or any Portfolio may be appropriate in the public interest or for the protection
of investors.
