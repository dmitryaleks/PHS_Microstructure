ARTICLE II
TRADING DAY SCHEDULE
SECTION 1. Trading Day and Non-Trading Day
Every day shall be a Trading Day except for Saturdays, Sundays, legal holidays, special
holidays, days when the Bangko Sentra/ ng Pi/ipinas (BSP) is closed, and such other days
as may otherwise be declared by the SEC or the Exchange, through its President or other
duly authorized representative, to be a non-Trading Day.

PRE-OPEN PERIOD
No matching of Orders can occur during this period, but
Trading Participants (T PS) can enter, modify, or cancel
Orders, which will be processed based on the pre-opening
algorithm.
PRE-OPEN NO-CANCEL PERIOD
TPs are allowed to enter Orders but cannot cancel or modify
Orders during this period.
OPENING PERIOD
The period when the Opening Price for all Securities is
calculated. During the Opening Period, the Order book is
frozen and Order entry, modification and cancellation by TPs
are not allowed.
CONTINUOUS TRADING
A period when Orders are automatically matched at the Best
Price in accordance with the Revised Trading Rules.
PRE-CLOSE AUCTION PERIOD
This period is the same as the Pre-Open Auction Period.
During this period, TPs can enter, modify or cancel Orders.
PRE-CLOSE NO-CANCEL PERIOD
During this period, TPs are allowed to enter Orders but they
cannot cancel or modify Orders.
RUN-OFF/ TRADING-A T-LAST
TPs can enter Limit Orders at the Closing Price only or Market
Orders but matching is executed only at the Closing Price for
both Order types.

IMPLEMENTATION OF EXTENDED PRE-CLOSE PERIOD
The Securities and Exchange Commission ("Commission"), in its letter dated September 26,
2013, informed the Philippine Stock Exchange ("Exchange"), that the Commission approved
the amendments to the Revised Trading Rules and Implementing Guidelines, specifically
the extension of the trading Pre-Close Period from three (3) minutes to five (5) minutes.
Accordingly, the Exchange will implement the following schedule for Trading Phase effective
November 4, 2013:
9:00 a.m.
9:30 a.m.
12:00 p.m.
1:30 p.m.
