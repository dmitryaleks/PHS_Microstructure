This clarifies the news report entitled Bourse backs out of PDS deal, Dpublished in the Philippine Daily Inquirer dated 16
April 2018, which stated, in part, that:
The Philippine Stock exchange (PSE) has officially backed out of the race to take over the Philippine Dealing System
Holdings (PDS Group), dashing hopes for the unification of the countrys capital market infrastructure.
As the timetable to close purchase agreements with key PDS shareholders lapsed on March 31 , the PSE wrote to the
counter-parties officially advising them that they would no longer seek an extension of the agreements, sources privy to
the transaction said.
This was after a board meeting was called to decide on the course of action for PDS, in which the majority decided to
allow the purchase agreements to lapse.
The PSE is content o be a 20-percent shareholder of PDS, Othe source said, adding that the bourse might even
eventually consider selling its stake to Landbank depending on the terms of the buyout offer.
The PSE is not the source of the report and there is no factual basis to attribute the items mentioned to the Exchange.
The PSE did not advise its counterparties to the Share Purchase Agreements (SPA) that PSE will no longer seek an
extension of the SPA. PSE is committed to its vision to unify the fixed-income and equities markets and enable all
stakeholders to reap the accruing benefits of an integrated capital market.
Discussions on whether to sell the 20% stake of PSE in PDS Holdings Corporation are premature given that PSE has not
formally received any offer for the purchase of the same.
