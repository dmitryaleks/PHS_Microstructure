Please be informed that the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) issued on June 5, 2024

amended by RR No. 1-2008, or the guidelines and conditions for the tax treatment of
securities borrowing and lending ("SBL") transactions involving shares of stock or
securities listed in PSE.

registration of Master Securities Lending Agreement ("MSLA") and Global Master
