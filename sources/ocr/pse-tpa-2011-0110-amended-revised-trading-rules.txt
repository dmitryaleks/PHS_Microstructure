ARTICLE II
TRADING DAY SCHEDULE
SECTION 1. Trading Day and Non-Trading Day
Every day shall be a Trading Day except for Saturdays, Sundays, legal holidays, special
holidays, days when the Bangko Sentra/ ng Pilipinas (BSP) is closed, and such other days
as may otherwise be declared by the SEC or the Exchange, through its President or other
duly authorized representative, to be a non-Trading Day.

ARTICLE IV
GENERAL TRADING OPERATIONS
SECTION 13. Order Modification

Modification of Orders shall be prohibited within a specific phase during Market Pre-
Open and Pre-Close Period and Market Recess in accordance with the Implementing
Guidelines of these Rules.
A posted and unmatched Order may be modified without losing its priority in the Order
queue if it involves any or a combination of the following:
i. a decrease in its volume;
ii. a modification of validity type; or
iii. a modification of client account code.
A modified Order loses its priority when it involves any or a combination of the following:
i. an increase in its volume;
ii. a modification of the limit price entered; or
iii. a modification of the trigger price entered.
No unmatched Order modification is allowed from client to proprietary or vice versa.
SECTION 14. Cancellations of Orders
a.
b.
c.
d.
Cancellation of Orders shall be prohibited within a specific phase during Market Pre-
Open and Pre-Close Period and Market Recess in accordance with the Implementing
Guidelines of these Rules.
Cancellation of any remaining unexecuted portion of a partially matched Order is
allowed.
The Exchange shall cancel the following Orders which are deemed invalid:
i. Orders for a certain Security in the event of corporate actions resulting to an
adjustment in the Closing Price;
ii. Orders on the ex-date for Securities with cash and/or property dividends; or
iii. Orders for Securities that cross a Board Lot.
The Trading Participant can only direct the Exchange to execute a cancellation of all the
Orders already posted when there occurs a system failure or interruption on the PSE-

ARTICLE VI
CROSS TRANSACTIONS AND BLOCK SALE
SECTION 3. Block Sales
a.
b.
c.

j.
Upon approval by the Exchange, Block Sales shall be immediately entered into the
Trading System of the Exchange.
Block Sales shall comply with the minimum transaction value and the allowable
execution prices in accordance with the Implementing Guidelines of these Rules.
Block Sales shall only constitute pre-arranged transactions between and among parties
to be executed through the Exchange.
Block Sales shall have an execution price of above or below a prescribed ratio of the
previous Trading Day's Closing Price or the ACP in the event of corporate actions, or the
last ACP in cases where there is no trading activity for the Security in the immediately
preceding Trading Day, in accordance with the Implementing Guidelines of these Rules.
Block Sales may involve one or two Trading Participants, but shall have different clients
for the buying and the selling side.
In Block Sales involving two Trading Participants, a Block Sale declaration which is not
confirmed by the counterpart Trading Participant shall be eliminated after the period set
by the Exchange.
Block Sales shall be settled based on Clearing Agency rules.
The Block Sale Market adopts the same Security state, other than freezing and
reservation, implemented in the Normal Market.
Block Sale transactions can be executed from the Market Pre-Open to the Trading-at-
Last/Run-Off period, including Market Recess.
Requests for Block Sales shall be acted upon within two (2) Trading Days.

ARTICLE VIII
MARKET HALT OR SUSPENSION OF TRADING ACTIVITY
SECTION 2. Market Halt
a.
b.
c.
d.
e.
The Exchange may halt the trading in the Market if at least one-third (1/3) of the Trading
Participant-users cannot access the Trading System due to Trading System problems
such as, but not limited to, server failure, network/link problems, or application errors.
In the event of Market Halt occurring thirty (30) minutes or more, the Exchange shall
extend trading hours. However, trading extension will no longer be applicable for whole
day trading, except for the purpose of completing the remaining market phases, if
warranted under the circumstances, in accordance with the implementing guidelines.
Resumption of trading shall be governed by the following guidelines:
i.
In the event a Market Halt affects the Pre-Open Period, the Exchange shall allot time
for the Pre-Open Period upon lifting of Market Halt.
ii. In the event a Market Halt affects the Pre-Close and/or Run-Off Period, upon lifting of
the Market Halt the Exchange shall complete the remaining market phases when
practicable.
In the event a Market Halt lasts for more than two (2) hours or fifteen (15) minutes
beyond the Run-Off Period, the Exchange has sole discretion to suspend or cancel the
trading for the day as the circumstances may warrant.
The President may authorize trading to proceed on any operational Trading Floor and
the affected Trading Participant may trade through its correspondent Trading Participant
or through any other mode of trading not prohibited by the rules of the Exchange.
