July 15, 2025
Per the Tax Advisory issued by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) on July 4, 2025, the
revised rate of the stock transaction tax [one-tenths of one percent (1/10 of 1%) of the
gross selling price or gross value in money] is still unavailable in BIR Form No. 2552 in
the eBIRForms and the Electronic Filing and Payment System (eFPS). Taxpayers are
therefore advised to manually file BIR Form No. 2552 and pay the taxes due at any
Authorized Agent Bank (AAB).
Attached for your reference is the said BIR Tax Advisory.
