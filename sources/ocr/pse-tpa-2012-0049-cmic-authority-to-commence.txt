Please be informed that the Securities Exchange Commission (SEC) issued an Order dated 9
March 2012 and received by CMIC on 12 March 2012, granting CMIC's request to commence
operation. The Order authorized CMIC "to discharge its function as the independent audit,
surveillance and compliance unit of PSE effective March 12, 2012."
Accordingly, the CMIC Rules as approved by SEC, posted in the PSE Website on 23
February 2012, shall be considered effective as of 12 March 2012.
