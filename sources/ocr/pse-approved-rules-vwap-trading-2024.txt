APPROVED RULES ON VWAP TRADING
February 01, 2024
Please be advised that the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) has approved the PSE
Rules on Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP) Trading ("VWAP Trading Rules"). Accordingly,
relevant provisions in the PSE Trading Rules and its Implementing Guidelines were amended to
incorporate the VWAP Trading Rules.
The approved VWAP Trading Rules, attached herewith as Annex "A," shall take effect
immediately.
The Exchange shall advise the public in due course of the official launch and implementation of
the VWAP Trading Rules.
For your information and guidance.

REVISED TRADING RULES
ARTICLE 1
DEFINITIONS AND CONSTRUCTIONS
SECTION 1. Definition of Terms
"VWAP Transaction" shall mean a pre-arranged transaction executed at the volume weighted
average price of a Security. A VWAP Trade is executed by a Trading Participant for different
clients or its proprietary account through the VWAP trading facility of the Exchange, subject to
the Exchange's discretion to subsequently make the VWAP trading facility available for two-firm
trading on a date to be determined by the Exchange and subject to prior approval of the
Commission.

ARTICLE VI
VWAP TRADING
SECTION 1. General Provisions
a. VWAP Trading shall be allowed by the Exchange.
b. It shall only be executed during the VWAP Trading session set by the Exchange.
c. All transactions executed during the VWAP Trading session shall be executed only at the
VWAP computed by the Exchange.
d. VWAP transactions shall be settled based on Clearing Agency Rules.
SECTION 2. Trading
a.
b.
c.
d.
e.
Unless otherwise provided herein, other provisions of the PSE Trading Rules shall apply
to VWAP Trading in the same manner as they apply to regular trades.
VWAP Trading shall comply with the conditions set out in the Implementing Guidelines
of these Rules.
VWAP Trading is not allowed for a currently suspended or halted Security.
Only authorized Salesmen/ Traders of the TP shall be allowed to execute VWAP
transactions.
The Order value limit set by the TP for each of its Salesmen/ Traders shall also apply in
VWAP Trading.

VWAP TRADING
1. VWAP transactions can only be executed through the trading facilities of the Exchange in
accordance with the following parameters:
a. The execution price shall be computed based on the volume-weighted average price for the
Security in a given Trading Day;
b. The VWAP shall not exceed four (4) decimal places; and
c. The value of the VWAP transaction shall be no less than PhP500,000.
2. Except Block Sales, intentional cross and Odd Lot transactions, all other trades shall be
included in the VWAP computation for the Security.
3. VWAP transactions can only be executed within fifteen (15) minutes after the Run-
off/ Trading-at-Last Period.
If there is delay in the display of VWAP of a Security, the Exchange shall suspend the Closing
VWAP Session and immediately make an announcement to the Trading Participants. If the
issue is resolved with at least five (5) minutes remaining in the Closing VWAP Session, the
Exchange will resume the Closing VWAP session. However, if the issue persists and there is
less than five (5) minutes remaining in the Closing VWAP Session, the Exchange shall cancel
VWAP trading for the day.
4. VWAP transaction may only be executed by a single Trading Participant, provided that the
Exchange shall have the option to subsequently make the VWAP trading facility available for
two-firm trading on a date to be determined by the Exchange and subject to prior approval of
the Commission.
5. VWAP transactions shall be included in the End-of-Day reports.
