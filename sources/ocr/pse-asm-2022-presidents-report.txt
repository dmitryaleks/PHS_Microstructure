Regulatory Enhancements Reforms
New Rules and Rule Enhancements
Preferred Shares Only Listing
PSE
14,
Georgo Morina I Poxols
Option to raise funds through IPO by issuing preferred shares, without
subsequent listing of common shares.
Viable mode of capital raising by PPPs/infrastructure companies who are
not yet ready to offer common shares to the public as full earnings
potential have not yet been reached.
Amendments to Backdoor Listing Rules
Puts in place more stringent measures in regulating reverse takeovers or
transactions leading to the backdoor listing of unlisted companies
Amendments to the Rule for Local Small Investors
Authorizes the Exchange to increase the maximum subscription amount
beyond Php100k, depending on the offer size of the IPO
Higher chance for issuers to achieve maximum LSI take-up

Regulatory Enhancements Reforms
New Rules and Rule Enhancements
Amendments to Lock-up Rule in Main and SME Boards
PSE
Allows alternative investment funds (AIFs) to sell their shares during the
IPO, subject to certain conditions.
This frees up capital for the AIFs to reinvest in other target firms.
Increases number of available shares to IPO investors
Lock-up Exemption and Stockholders' Equity Requirement for REITs
Exempts REIT sponsors from the lock-up requirement to enable
secondary offering of REIT shares during the IPO.
Compliance with the Php500 million stockholders' equity requirement shall
be determined at the time of filing of listing application
