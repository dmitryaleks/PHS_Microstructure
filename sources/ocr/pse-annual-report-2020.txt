l. BUSINESS AND GENERAL INFORMATION
Item 1. Business
Description of Registrant
The Philippine Stock Exchange, Inc. was incorporated on July 14, 1992 as a non-stock
corporation and later became a stock corporation on August 3, 2001. On December 15, 2003,
pursuant to the demutualization mandate of Republic Act No. 8799, or the Securities Regulation
Code (SRC), the Company's outstanding capital stock was listed by way of introduction. On
February 18, 2004, the Company sold 6,077,505 shares from its unissued stock to five strategic
investors by way of private placement that was approved by the Securities and Exchange
Commission (SEC), The strategic investors were the PLDT Beneficial Trust Fund, SMC
Retirement Fund} Government Service Insurance System, Kim Eng Investment, Ltd., and KE
Strategic Pte. Ltd.
The Company's revenues are primarily derived from listing-related and trading-related
fees. The Company charges listing fees for initial public offerings and additional listings, and for
annual listing maintenance. Meanwhile, trading revenues are primarily comprised of transaction
fees, subscription fees, and service fees related to clearing and settlement. Other sources of
revenue are membership, data feed, and miscellaneous fees.
Description of Subsidiaries and Affiliates
The Securities Clearing Corporation of the Philippines (SCCP) is a wholly-owned
subsidiary organized primarily as a clearing and settlement agency for SCCP-eligible trades
executed through the facilities of the Company. SCCP is responsible for: (a) synchronizing the
settlement of funds for transactions of clearing members and the transfer of securities through
Delivery versus Payment (DVP) clearing; (b) the administration of the Clearing and Trade
Guaranty Fund (CTGF) and guaranteeing the settlement of trades through the implementation
of its Fails Management System in the event of a Trading Participant's default; and (c)
performance of Risk Management and Monitoring to ensure final and irrevocable settlement.
The Capital Markets Integrity Corporation (CMIC) is also a wholly-owned subsidiary. It
functions as the independent auditi surveillance and compliance unit of the Company, having
taken over the functions of the former Market Regulation Division of the Company. CMIC
operates to ensure the integrity of the capital market and has jurisdiction to investigate all
violations of the SRC and CMIC Rules by Trading Participants, and investigate trading-related
irregularities and unusual trading activities committed by Issuers. The SEC granted CMIC its
authority to operate as the independent audit, surveillance and compliance unit of the Company
with provisional selfcgulatory organization (SRO) status on February 2, 2012.
The Company is also a shareholder of the Philippine Dealing System Holdings
Corporation (PDSHC), the holding company of the Philippine Dealing & Exchange Corporation
(PDEX), otherwise known as the Fixed Income Exchange (FIE), the Philippine Depository &
Trust Corp. (PDTC) and the Philippines Securities Settlement Corporation (PSSC). As of
February 29, 2016, the Company is a stockholder of record of 1,311 ,439 shares, or 20.98%, of
all of the issued and outstanding shares.

percentage of hours spent on the relevant activities of the departments performing the

Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT)
The Department of Finance (DOF), SEC, Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR), and the
Company launched the final amendments to the regulations governing REITs in a joint
signing ceremony on January 20, 2020. This favorable development came as a result of the
Company's and market regulators} conscious efforts in the past few years to clear the
hurdles which have long deterred issuers from listing a REIT. Subsequent to the approval of
the Amended Listing Rules for REITs, the Company published its Guidelines for Broker
Eligibility to Trade REIT Securities on July 15, 2020. Similar to dollar denominated securities,
trading of REIT securities requires trading participants to attend a REIT training session
conducted by the Company and must issue a sworn certification to the Company attesting to
its operational readiness. The TP shall also comply with the applicable requirements under
the Name on Central Depository facility of the Philippine Depository Trust Corp, As of date,
there are 121 eligible TPs to trade REIT securities.
