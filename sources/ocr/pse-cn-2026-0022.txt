MEMO FOR EQUITY DEPOSITORY PARTICIPANTS No. 03 2026
SUBJECT: UPDATES ON PDTC LENDING AGENCY FOR PSE SBL PROGRAM:
ONBOARDED LENDERS AND BORROWERS WITH BIR-REGISTERED AGREEMENTS
22 APRIL 2026
DATE:
PDTC Lending Agent for PSE SBL Program has successfully onboarded Lenders and Borrowers with
BIR-registered agreements.
Onboarded lenders with securities to lend out may access PDTC SBL system anytime and
input lending instructions for the identified securities to be included in the Lending Pool.
Onboarded borrowers with BIR-registered agreements and opened cash collateral bank
accounts may access PDTC SBL system to check for available securities in the Lending Pool
and initiate a borrowing request for the identified security, provided cash collateral is
sufficient.
If lending or borrowing on behalf of your clients, please ensure that necessary internal agreements
with your clients are in place.
For Depository Participants interested in availing of PDTC's Lending Agency Service to participate
in the Lending Pool, please submit a Letter of Intent indicating your BP ID and the role being
applied for (either borrower, lender or both).
Upon receipt of the Letter of Intent, our Client Services unit will contact you to schedule your
users for the systems training. Following the completion of systems training, Borrower/Lender Kits
containing the template agreements and forms shall be provided.
The main documentation included in the Kit are the following agreements:
For Lender: Securities Lending Authorization Agreement (SLAA)
- between PDTC Lending Agent and Lender
For Borrower: Master Securities Lending Agreement (MSLA) / Accession Agreement
- between PDTC Lending Agent and Borrower; this agreement must be registered with the
BIR under the SEC and PSE SBL Rules
Please note that for borrowers, PDTC would also facilitate the opening of a separate cash
collateral bank account with the designated cash collateral bank.
