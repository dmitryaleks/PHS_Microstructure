2026 REVISED GUIDELINES FOR MASTER SECURITIES
LENDING AGREEMENT CLEARANCE
May 22, 2026
Please be advised that The Philippine Stock Exchange, Inc.'s ("PSE") 2026 Revised
Guidelines for Master Securities Lending Agreement ("MSLA") Clearance ("Revised
MSLA Guidelines") have been approved by the Securities and Exchange Commission
("SEC") on May 15, 2026 and shall take effect immediately.
The salient revisions are as follows:
1. PSE will function as a one-stop shop for MSLA and Accession Agreement pre-clearance
and BIR registration.
The PSE will act as a one-stop shop between the applicant (i.e., Borrowers or
Lending Agent) and the Bureau of Internal Revenue ("BIR"). As such, PSE will:

Removal of SEC's MSLA pre-clearance process.

If there are any deficiencies in the submissions or items for clarification, PSE shall notify the Applicant
via email. PSE may extend the review/ processing period if Applicant fails to respond or comply within
the prescribed time.
Upon satisfactory completion of the review by PSE, PSE shall issue a Pre-Clearance Certificate
indicating the MSLA Reference Number and transmit a copy thereof, together with the pre-cleared
documentary requirements, to the BIR and furnish a copy to the SEC. PSE shall also remit the BIR
registration fee to the BIR.
For Accession Agreements, submission to the BIR will be done weekly, every Monday immediately
following the week when the Accession Agreement and other requirements are submitted (or the
immediately succeeding business day, if Monday falls on a regular holiday or a special non-working
day). The Applicant must submit the requirements no later than Wednesday, 12 noon, to be included
in the latest batch that will be submitted to the BIR.
Upon successful registration of the MSLA with the BIR, PSE shall receive the Certificate of
Registration/ Tax Exemption and release it to the Applicant. Upon successful filing of the Accession
Agreement with the BIR, PSE shall receive the new Certificate of Registration/ Tax Exemption
reflecting the additional borrower/ s, release it to the Lending Agent, and furnish a copy to the
Borrower/ s. The Borrower/ s may request for a certified true copy of the Certificate of Registration/ Tax
Exemption from the BIR for a minimal fee.
The Applicant shall provide a copy of the BIR Certificate of Registration/ Tax Exemption to the Capital
Markets Integrity Corporation within fifteen (15) days from issuance thereof.
The SEC may conduct post-audit review of any BIR-registered MSLA, GMSLA or Accession Agreement
based on SEC's audit calendar and impose sanctions and penalties against any person or entity found
to have violated any provision of SEC Memorandum Circular No. 7, series of 2006, or the SEC Rules
on Securities Borrowing and Lending, and any amendment or supplement thereto, pursuant to SEC's
regulatory powers and in accordance with SEC's rules and regulations.
All provisions of previous PSE SBL Guidelines for MSLA Clearance inconsistent herewith are hereby
amended accordingly.
