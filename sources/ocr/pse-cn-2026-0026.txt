The Exchange is inviting all interested parties to submit comments on the proposed
amendments to the PSE Market Making Rules.
The proposed rules aim to enhance market liquidity by introducing a general framework
applied uniformly to all market-making activities on the Exchange, while adopting tailored
operational frameworks for specific product categories such as Exchange-Traded Funds and
Global Philippine Depositary Receipts.
The proposed rules also set out the accreditation criteria for Market Makers and quoting
obligations such as maximum bid-ask spreads, minimum quote sizes, and required presence
times during trading sessions.
Lastly, the proposed rules permit an incentive framework designed to reward compliant
market makers for providing continuous liquidity.

Proposed Amendments to the PSE Market Making Rules
SECTION 1. Introduction
These Market Making Rules (hereinafter referred to as "Rules") are issued to govern the
accreditation, obligations, and reportorial requirements of Market Makers ("MMs") with specific
regulations for different Exchange-traded products or securities, as indicated in the Annexes to
these Rules, and as may be amended by the Exchange from time to time.
To contribute to the maintenance of fair and orderly markets and maintain active markets in
exchange-traded products, Market Makers must enter Market Making orders when there exists,
or it is reasonably anticipated that there will exist, a lack of price continuity or a temporary
disparity between the supply of, or demand for, a particular product.
SECTION 2. Definitions
When used in these Rules, the following terms shall have the meaning indicated below, unless
provided otherwise:
Commission — Refers to the Securities and Exchange Commission.
Designated Specialist — A licensed Salesman employed by a Market Maker and accredited by
the Exchange to perform Market Making obligations.
Exchange or PSE — Shall mean The Philippine Stock Exchange, Inc.
Issuer — Any legal entity issuing a financial instrument for which market-making activities are
permitted under these Rules.
Market Maker - A registered broker dealer that assumes the obligation of providing two-way
quotes for the purpose of ensuring liquidity and maintaining a fair and orderly trading market
for exchange-traded products eligible for market making.
"Market Making" - Means the continuous provision of liquidity by a Market Maker through the
simultaneous posting of two-way quotes, executed in compliance with the Exchange Rules and
applicable regulatory requirements.
Other capitalized terms used in these Rules but not otherwise defined in this section or elsewhere
in the Rules shall have the meaning ascribed to them in the relevant rules and regulations of the
Commission, Exchange, Securities Clearing Corporation of the Philippines ("SCCP"), or Capital
Markets Integrity Corporation ("CMIC"), as the case may be.

Market Maker will be reported to the Commission.

SECTION 7. Obligations of a Market Maker
a. All Market Makers must comply with the guidelines and parameters established by the
Exchange to ensure sufficient market liquidity. These guidelines define the applicable
quoting periods, spread parameters, minimum quote sizes, and the overall compliance
methodology:
(i) Maximum Spread
A Market Maker must ensure two-way quotes, i.e., buy and sell orders, with a maximum
spread. The limit of the bid and ask spread shall be based on the terms indicated by the
Exchange for a specific financial product or security.
(ii) Minimum Order Quantity
Each Market Making order posted by a Market Maker must have a minimum quantity
based on the terms indicated by the Exchange for a specific financial product or security.
The Exchange may prescribe other appropriate volume requirements, as may be necessary.

SECTION 8. Occurrence of a Wide Spread
a.
b.
Wide Spread shall mean the occurrence of any of the following during the Continuous
Trading phase:
(i) the bid and ask spread is larger than a limit specified by the Exchange from time to
time;
(ii) there is only one-sided quotation; or
(iii) there is no existing quotation on both the bid and ask queues.
Upon occurrence of Wide Spread, a Market Maker shall be obliged to perform the
following:
enter buy and/or sell Market Making orders into the System in quantities not less
than the minimum volume required by the Exchange to eliminate Wide Spread;
(ii) observe a maximum bid and offer spread throughout a mandatory period; and
(iii) maintain presence in the market within the period required by the Exchange.
The Exchange may periodically assess and revise the parameters to adjust to market conditions
such as, but not limited to, excessive volatility of the relevant securities.
SECTION 9. Responsibility for Market Making Orders
a. A Market Maker shall be fully responsible and liable for all Market Making orders entered
into the Trading System. Upon entering a Market Making order, the Market Maker
warrants to the Exchange that the said order is valid, binding and enforceable and that the
necessary authorizations and approval have been secured.

a.

The Market Maker shall maintain proper segregation of functions within the firm to prevent
the flow of information between its Designated Specialists and its other salesmen and any
conflict of interest that may result.
The Market Maker shall ensure that its Designated Specialist is able to enter orders or
handle or manage any transactions relating to client accounts or to any other account
without compromising the segregation of information between accounts.
A Market Maker shall use its designated Market Making Account only for entering Market
Making orders.
A Market Maker shall maintain only the Market Making and error accounts for the financial
product/s or securities in which it is designated as a Market Maker.
The Market Maker shall maintain complete and identifiable books and records for its
Market Making transactions. The Market Maker shall include a registry pinpointing the
person who acted as the Designated Specialist(s) during a particular day or period.

Such other cases as the Exchange may deem necessary for a fair and orderly market and to

the Exchange Rules shall likewise be complied with by the Issuer.

SECTION 12. Voluntary Termination of a Designated Specialist
The Exchange may, upon the written request of the Market Maker, terminate the accreditation of
its Designated Specialist, provided the Market Maker immediately appoints a replacement, if
necessary.
SECTION 13. Voluntary Withdrawal of Accreditation as Market Maker
a.
b.
c.
d.
A Market Maker may voluntarily withdraw as a Market Maker for any financial product or
security with which it has an existing Market Making agreement provided it submits to the
Exchange at least thirty (30) trading days prior to the intended date of withdrawal: (i) a
written request for its withdrawal; and (ii) the written consent of the concerned Issuer.
If the Market Maker intends to completely cease its Market Making operations, it shall
submit to the Exchange at least thirty (30) trading days prior to the intended date of
cessation: (i) a written request for its cessation; and (ii) the written consent of all Issuers
with which it has existing Market Making agreements.
A Market Maker which submits both requirements shall be eligible to apply for re-
accreditation as a Market Maker at any time. If it submits only the request for withdrawal
or cessation but fails to submit the written consent of the concerned Issuers, it shall only be
eligible to apply for re-accreditation as a Market Maker after a cooling-off period of one (1)
month from the effective date of withdrawal or cessation.
Within a reasonable time and in all cases prior to the intended date of withdrawal by the
Market Maker or its intended date of cessation of Market Making operations, the Issuer
shall submit to the Exchange the name and other details of the replacement Market Maker
appointed by the Issuer. In cases where the previous Market Maker is the sole Market
Maker of a financial product or security, the appointment of the replacement Market Maker
shall commence not later than the effective date of withdrawal or the effective date of the
cessation of the Market Making operations of the previous Market Maker.
SECTION 14. Consequences of Non-Compliance with Market Making Obligations
In the event of continuous failure by the Market Maker to comply with its Market Making
obligations under these Rules, the PSE may contact and request a briefing from the Market Maker
which may result in the suspension or revocation of a firm's Market Maker status.
The foregoing shall be without prejudice to any other regulatory action that the Exchange may
take against the Market Maker or its Designated Specialist, as may be applicable under the
circumstances.
