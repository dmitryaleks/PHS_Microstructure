# Navigate PSE's Rulebook Before Eqlipse Lands

The Philippine Stock Exchange equity market on 6 October 2026 is a single venue, a single clearing house and a single depository running a June 2010 rulebook amended by circulars, and for a hedge fund its microstructure reduces to a short list of facts that break ported algorithms. Client-side algorithms cannot use direct market access ([DMA Rules](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/SEC-Approved-DMA-Rules.pdf#page=7)); the official close is struck at 14:50 and nothing can be cancelled after 14:48 ([Approved Rules on VWAP Trading](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2024/05/Approved-Rules-on-VWAP-Trading.pdf#page=6)); a price breach freezes the stock for every participant instead of triggering an auction ([Implementing Guidelines](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/Implementing-Guidelines-of-the-Revised-Trading-Rules.pdf#page=26)); a PHP 10m round trip costs **70.12 bp** at a 25 bp commission, 10 bp of it a seller-only tax ([CN-2025-0028](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2025-0028.pdf#page=1); [Investing at PSE](https://www.pse.com.ph/investing-at-pse/)); and short selling, legal since 6 November 2023, has never reported a single share ([Daily Short Selling Report, 5 Oct 2026](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/10/October-05-2026.pdf#page=2)). Liquidity is thin and lumpy: **PHP 7.71bn a day** (about US$120m), with block sales that no participation algorithm can trade equal to **18.4%** of 2026 value ([PSE Weekly Report](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/10/page01_PSEWeeklyReport2026_wk40.pdf#page=1); computed from [PSE daily reports](https://www.pse.com.ph/market-report/)). The date to design around is **Monday 23 November 2026**, when Nasdaq Eqlipse is scheduled to replace PSEtrade XTS in a big-bang cut-over with no parallel run and limit orders only on Day 1 ([NTE broker forum](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/07/PSE-New-Trading-Engine-TradeX-Back-office_Broker-Forum_07092026-1.pdf#page=9); [NTE FAQ](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/08/Frequent-Asked-Questions.pdf#page=1)), while the rule changes tied to it (one-share lots, a new tick table, a changed run-off rule, Negotiated Trades) were still "For SEC Approval" or "Revising per Public Comments" on PSE's last status of 17 August ([PSE 1H 2026 briefing](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/08/2026.08.17-PSE-STAR-1H-2026.pdf#page=9)). Every finding is as of 6 October 2026; the main gaps in the public record are the new engine's specifications, the SEC's decisions, whether executions carry broker identifiers, and any measured spread, depth or auction-volume statistic. The design response is to version-gate every parameter by trade date and engine and to treat the cut-over date as a target with execution risk ([Chapter 16](../content/16-execution-implications.md)).

## Algorithms must live at the broker in a one-venue, one-CCP market

One group owns the whole chain. The PSE is the only stock exchange and a front-line self-regulator, owns 100% of the clearing house SCCP and of its surveillance arm CMIC, and since December 2024 has bought control of the PDS group whose subsidiary PDTC is the depository, 94.55% beneficially owned on 4 February 2026 ([PSE annual report 2025](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/04/2025-Annual-Report.pdf#page=9); [PSE 17-C, 4 Feb 2026](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/03/17-C-04-February-2026-Signing-of-Agreement-to-purchase-8146-PDSHC-shares-owned-by-PDIC-and-13146-shares-held-by-PDIC-in-trust-for-Export-and-Industry-Bank-Inc.pdf#page=3)). There is no venue selection, routing or cross-market problem, so every optimisation is intraday scheduling, broker selection and counterparty management ([Chapter 1](../content/01-market-architecture.md)). The market is also small: 387 live security lines for 280 issuers, of which 285 are common shares (eight of them REITs), 98 preferreds, two PDRs, one warrant series and one ETF, with 65 lines suspended, no listed derivative and no single-stock market maker ([PSE security frames](https://frames.pse.com.ph/security/FMETF); [Chapter 2](../content/02-instruments.md)). Since 11 August 2026 a free-float breach brings an immediate suspension of up to six months and then automatic delisting, with no cure period in which the stock keeps trading ([PSE minimum-public-ownership memo](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/08/Memo-to-Public_Effectivity-of-the-Amended-MPO-Rule-Rvsd-PO-Guidelines-v2.pdf#page=6)).

Orders reach the engine only through 123 trading participants (121 active), a concentrated and thinly capitalised layer: the top ten took 64.99% of broker-ranked value on 5 October 2026 and the four foreign-owned firms 29.03% ([PSE participant directory](https://www.pse.com.ph/directory/); [PSE broker ranking](https://frames.pse.com.ph/brokerRankings)). Brokers fail. EquitiWorld was suspended on an SEC order in October 2024, and the SEC approved its customer allocation plan only on 17 March 2026 ([CN-2024-0053](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2024-0053.pdf#page=1); [CN-2026-0012](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2026-0012.pdf#page=2)). PSE proposes lifting unimpaired paid-up capital to at least PHP 50m by end-2027 and PHP 100m by end-2029 ([PSE memo, July 2026](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/07/Memo-to-Public_Proposed-Increase-in-the-Unimpaired-Paid-Up-Capital-of-TP-1.pdf#page=3)); the point for a fund is that exposure runs to the clearing member first and to SCCP only after it.

Orders travel from a PSE-certified front end or the PSETradeX terminal to the Common Customer Gateway over unencrypted FIX 5.0 SP1 and into PSEtrade XTS, which has matched the market since 22 June 2015 ([PSE FIX specification v2.5](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/01/PSE_FIX_Specification_v2_5-10-August-2015.pdf#page=8); [PSE annual report 2015](https://ir.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2020/04/2015-PSE-Annual-Report.pdf#page=42)). The DMA Rules are blunt that "the DMA Facility shall not be used for High-Frequency and/or Algorithmic trading", and Sponsored Access is for qualified institutional buyers only; PSE's September 2023 proposal to allow algorithms (a manually entered parent, limit-order children) has no approval on record, although PSE says it has granted exemptions for child orders of conditioned parent orders ([DMA Rules](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/SEC-Approved-DMA-Rules.pdf#page=7); [DMA Rules, services](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/SEC-Approved-DMA-Rules.pdf#page=3); [CN-2023-0043](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2023-0043.pdf#page=3)). There is no co-location, no published message-rate limit and one front-end log-in per participant, and PSE reserves the right to impose message-flow controls ([Implementing Guidelines](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/Implementing-Guidelines-of-the-Revised-Trading-Rules.pdf#page=31)). The working inference is that institutional algorithms run inside the broker's certified front end with human-entered parents and limit-order children, which makes the broker's exemption status, message limits and per-order value limit (the Exchange's own cap is unpublished) the first things to settle ([Chapter 13](../content/13-regulatory-constraints.md)).

Reliability is a design input. Four technical disruptions in 2022–2025 (the full-day cancellation of 4 January 2022 when 43 of 125 participants could not connect, a halt from 09:32 to 11:56 on 3 January 2024, and late opens at 09:55 on 9 December 2024 and 11:10 on 24 March 2025) trace, where a cause was stated, to the connectivity or front-end layer rather than to matching ([CN-2022-0001](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2022-0001.pdf#page=1); [CN-2024-0003](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2024-0003.pdf#page=1); [CN-2024-0061](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2024-0061.pdf#page=1); [CN-2025-0015](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2025-0015.pdf#page=1)). Since 20 August 2025 a market-wide halt needs participants holding more than half of six-month trading value to be unable to trade even through their mandatory correspondent, and a PAGASA wind signal of 3 or higher over Metro Manila at 06:00 cancels the day ([CN-2025-0037](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2025-0037.pdf#page=1); [typhoon rule](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2025-0037.pdf#page=4)).

## A 2010 rulebook sets the clock: a 14:50 close, a 14:48 cancel limit and a freeze instead of an auction

The market has one whole-day session with two call auctions and a recess.

| PHT | Phase | What participants can do |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | Pre-open | Enter, amend, cancel; no matching |
| 09:15 | Pre-open no-cancel | Enter only |
| 09:30:00 | Open | Uncross at the scheduled second; continuous trading follows |
| 12:00–13:00 | Recess | No entry, amendment or cancellation |
| 14:45 | Pre-close | Enter, amend, cancel |
| 14:48 | Pre-close no-cancel | Enter only |
| 14:50 | Run-off | Orders only at the closing price struck at 14:50 |
| 15:00–15:15 | Closing VWAP session | VWAP-only trades, at least PHP 500,000, one participant |

The times are RTR Article II as restated on 1 February 2024 ([Approved Rules on VWAP Trading](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2024/05/Approved-Rules-on-VWAP-Trading.pdf#page=3)). Two windows are hard: the last cancel is 09:14:59 and 14:47:59, and nothing at all can be entered in the recess. The closing price is set at 14:50 by the same five-step rule as the open (maximum matched volume, least unmatched, market pressure, nearest the reference, the reference itself), which can be replicated exactly because PSE's six worked examples are published test vectors ([Implementing Guidelines](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/Implementing-Guidelines-of-the-Revised-Trading-Rules.pdf#page=13)); there is no random end, no extension and no volatility auction. The run-off executes only at that closing price, so run-off flow cannot move it; today XTS rejects an incoming order if a better-priced passive order rests, whereas PSE's draft for Eqlipse would accept and match it at the closing price ([CN-2025-0046](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2025-0046.pdf#page=5)). The 15:00–15:15 Closing VWAP session, live since 1 March 2024, is not a continuation of trading: it takes only VWAP trades with one participant on both sides, and it traded on 67 of 186 days in 2026, 0.12% of value ([CN-2024-0012](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2024-0012.pdf#page=1); computed from [PSE daily reports](https://www.pse.com.ph/market-report/)). A pipeline still assuming the 15:30 close that ended on 13 March 2020 is wrong ([CN-2022-0009](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2022-0009.pdf#page=1); [Chapter 3](../content/03-sessions.md), [Chapter 6](../content/06-auctions.md)).

Order handling is simpler than the rulebook suggests and more fragile. The rulebook defines limit, market, market-to-limit, stop, market-on-open/close and cross orders, but a market order executes at the best bid or offer or the last price, whichever is better, with its remainder "queued ... for immediate execution", and on Eqlipse's first day only limit orders will exist ([Revised Trading Rules](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/Revised-Trading-Rules.pdf#page=21); [NTE FAQ](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/08/Frequent-Asked-Questions.pdf#page=1)). Priority is price then time, lost on a re-price or size increase and kept on a size decrease or validity change ([Revised Trading Rules](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/Revised-Trading-Rules.pdf#page=24)). There is no self-trade prevention, and a participant's new counterpart order matches its own earlier order regardless of queue position; no cancel-on-disconnect is documented; and the Exchange cancels resting orders on dividend ex-dates, which have fallen one trading day before the record date since 24 August 2023 ([Revised Trading Rules](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/Revised-Trading-Rules.pdf#page=31); [PSE FIX specification](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/01/PSE_FIX_Specification_v2_5-10-August-2015.pdf#page=14); [Revised Trading Rules](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/Revised-Trading-Rules.pdf#page=25); [CN-2023-0031](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2023-0031.pdf#page=1); [Chapter 4](../content/04-order-types.md)).

Tick and lot come from one 15-band table keyed on the previous close and unchanged since 26 July 2010, with lots from 1,000,000 shares down to 5 and ticks from PHP 0.0001 to 5.00 ([Revised Trading Rules](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/Revised-Trading-Rules.pdf#page=21)). Above PHP 5 the tick is 4–25 bp of price and it jumps 2.5 times at PHP 20, 500 and 5,000: crossing a one-tick spread on PHP 10m costs 5.0 bp at a 19.98 reference and 12.5 bp at 20.00 (computed from the table). One Lot One Share, a ten-band tick table and the end of the odd-lot market are proposals, not rules ([CN-2025-0046](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2025-0046.pdf#page=3)).

Price controls are a three-layer stack that freezes rather than auctions. A static band of +50% and −30% of the reference price has applied since 24 March 2020 ([CN-2020-0028](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2020-0028.pdf#page=1)); a dynamic threshold caps the jump from the last traded price at 20%, 15% or 10% by six-month trade count ([TPA 2026-0036](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/TPA-2026-0036.pdf#page=1)); and an order that would breach either freezes the security for every participant, with no posting, amending or cancelling until Market Control acts, normally within five minutes ([Implementing Guidelines](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/Implementing-Guidelines-of-the-Revised-Trading-Rules.pdf#page=26)). A PSEi fall of 10%, 15% or 20% halts the market for 15, 30 or 60 minutes ([CN-2020-0044](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2020-0044.pdf#page=2)), a halt keeps resting orders and a suspension purges them ([Implementing Guidelines](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/Implementing-Guidelines-of-the-Revised-Trading-Rules.pdf#page=27)), and the floor compounds: after three floor sessions a stock stands at 34.3% of its reference ([Chapter 7](../content/07-price-controls.md)). Pre-arranged size is routed by rule: a deal of at least PHP 20m within ±5% of the previous close must go through the Block Sale Market, a special block from PHP 50m needs COO approval, and smaller deals can only cross inside the best bid and offer ([Implementing Guidelines](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/Implementing-Guidelines-of-the-Revised-Trading-Rules.pdf#page=23); [CN-2026-0031](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2026-0031.pdf#page=3)). The guidelines say regular blocks settle on the execution date while the market settles T+2, and SCCP's 2018 procedures exclude blocks from its guarantee, an unresolved gap ([SCCP Operating Procedures](https://www.sccp.com.ph/resources/files/rules/SCCP_Revised_OpProcs_-_Approved_by_the_SEC_031318.pdf#page=23); [Chapter 5](../content/05-matching.md)).

## A PHP 7.7bn day is thinner, lumpier and more event-driven than headline volume suggests

Value traded averaged PHP 7.71bn a day in 2026 to 2 October, but the regular market an algorithm can reach was PHP 6.25bn against a PHP 7.56bn headline over January–August, because block sales and odd lots are 17–19% of value ([PSE Weekly Report](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/10/page01_PSEWeeklyReport2026_wk40.pdf#page=1); [PSE Monthly Report, August 2026](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/09/08-2026_PSE-Monthly-Report_Preview.pdf#page=1)). Blocks print outside the stock's own volume but inside turnover: 12.4% of value on the median 2026 day and 82.2% on 11 September, when one FGEN block was PHP 25.8bn ([PSE Daily Quotation Report, 11 Sep 2026](https://documents.pse.com.ph/market_report/September%2011,%202026-EOD.pdf#page=11); computed from [PSE daily reports](https://www.pse.com.ph/market-report/)). Turnover is concentrated: the ten most-traded names are about 60% of regular value on a median day and only about 14 names trade PHP 100m or more, so a PHP 1bn parent order is roughly 13% of the whole market's average day (computed from [PSE daily reports](https://www.pse.com.ph/market-report/); [Chapter 15](../content/15-empirical.md)). Quote quality is not published, but end-of-day books give a proxy: the median spread is 31 bp for the ten most-traded names, 87 bp for ranks 61–100 and 227 bp beyond, and 33–44% of liquid-name spreads equal exactly one tick, so queue position matters more than price improvement (computed from [PSE daily reports](https://www.pse.com.ph/market-report/)).

The close is not a neutral reference. The PSEi closed at the day's high on 21.3% and at its low on 16.8% of 1,813 sessions since 2019, against 1–8% for a random walk; in the ten most liquid stocks the close lands on the day's high or low about five times as often as chance; and on the last session of a month the share of top-100 stocks closing at an extreme is 43.8% against 34.0% on ordinary days (computed from [PSE weekly](https://www.pse.com.ph/market-report/) and [daily reports](https://www.pse.com.ph/market-report/)). Event days dominate flow. MSCI implementation days traded a median 3.04 times the prior 20-day median value, 11 of 12 at twice normal or more, with net foreign selling on 10 of 12, and FTSE third-Friday days about 2.1 times; one deleted stock can be 18% to 38% of the market's value that day, and CBC rose 38.9% on 89 times its median value before its PSEi inclusion and then gave back 6.1% (computed from [PSE daily reports](https://www.pse.com.ph/market-report/); [MSCI Philippines factsheet](https://www.msci.com/documents/10199/255599/msci-philippines-index.pdf#page=1)). The Philippines had only nine MSCI Standard constituents on 30 September 2026, with ICTSI at 44.73%, and the November implementation may fall in the Eqlipse go-live week ([Chapter 11](../content/11-market-data.md)).

Foreigners are on 49.03% of trade sides in 2026 and have been net sellers every year since 2022, PHP 31.5bn so far this year, while retail is roughly 18–19% of value ([PSE Weekly Report](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/10/page01_PSEWeeklyReport2026_wk40.pdf#page=1); [PSE 2026 President's Report](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/07/PSE-ASM-2026-PRESIDENT_S-REPORT.pdf#page=7)). Nobody is obliged to quote: the only market-making regime covers one ETF, FMETF, and caps its spread at ten ticks at its price band with quotes of at least five board lots, so FMETF closed at 97.00 bid and 97.50 ask on 2 October, exactly the ceiling ([PSE ETF Rules](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/PSE-ETF-RULES-A-B-C-for-Website.pdf#page=22); [PSE Daily Quotation Report, 2 Oct 2026](https://documents.pse.com.ph/market_report/October%2002,%202026-EOD.pdf#page=9)). Two market-making drafts, PSE's and the SEC's, are not in force ([CN-2026-0026](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2026-0026.pdf#page=4); [CN-2026-0039](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/08/CN-No.-2026-0039.pdf#page=13); [Chapter 8](../content/08-market-making.md)).

Shorting and hedging exist on paper only. Short selling has been live since 6 November 2023 in 52 eligible names under a 10% short-interest cap, an uptick test, day-order-only and borrow-before-sale rules, yet all 707 distinct Daily Short Sell Reports through 5 October 2026 show zero volume ([PSE Short Selling Guidelines](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2024/05/PSE-Guidelines-on-Short-Selling-Transactions_Oct2023_v3.pdf#page=2); [Daily Short Selling Report, 5 Oct 2026](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/10/October-05-2026.pdf#page=2)). The plumbing is partial: PDTC's lending pool onboarded its first participants in April–May 2026 with cash collateral only, the PSE became the one-stop clearance point for lending agreements on 15 May 2026, and the foreign-lender route filed on 16 April 2026 still awaited SEC approval on 17 August ([CN-2026-0022](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2026-0022.pdf#page=1); [MSLA clearance guidelines](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/05/2026-Revised-Guidelines-for-MSLA-Clearance_SEC-approved.pdf#page=2); [PSE 1H 2026 briefing](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/08/2026.08.17-PSE-STAR-1H-2026.pdf#page=9)). No derivative on PSE equities is listed, so index hedges run offshore, for example in the US-listed EPHE (about US$132m of assets, 40 holdings) ([PSE 1H 2026 briefing](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/08/2026.08.17-PSE-STAR-1H-2026.pdf#page=10); [EPHE data page](https://stockanalysis.com/etf/ephe/); [Chapter 9](../content/09-short-selling.md)).

## Net returns hinge on a 10 bp sell-side tax, a noon deadline and foreign room

The explicit cost stack is short and mostly fixed. Commission is negotiated under a 1.5% cap with no regulatory minimum since 18 April 2024; the PSE fee is 0.005% plus VAT, the SCCP fee 0.01% and the SEC "SRC fee" 0.005% (listed in PSE's own fee illustration but with no primary rate document); and the stock transaction tax is 0.1% of the gross selling price, seller only and set by trade date, since 1 July 2025, against 0.6% from 2018 ([CN-2024-0029](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2024/09/Removal-of-Minimum-Commission-Chanrges_CN-2024-0029.pdf#page=1); [Investing at PSE](https://www.pse.com.ph/investing-at-pse/); [CN-2025-0028](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2025-0028.pdf#page=1); [RA 12214](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2025/ra_12214_2025.html)). A round trip therefore costs **2.24c + 14.12 bp** for a commission of c basis points: PHP 70,120 on PHP 10m at 25 bp, against PHP 120,120 before July 2025, and a book that turns over 20 times a year pays about 2% of capital in tax alone because the tax is on gross value and is never netted ([Chapter 12](../content/12-costs.md)). Investor-level tax matters less for trading than for holding: listed shares carry no capital-gains tax, but non-resident corporate funds withhold 25% on cash dividends (15% if the home country credits 10 points or taxes nothing), 10% on REIT dividends and 25% on PHP interest, and PSE's own fee page still prints the pre-2021 figure of 30% ([BIR RR 21-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20NO.%2021-2025.pdf#page=7); [CREATE Act](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2021/pdf/ra_11534_2021.pdf#page=12); [Investing at PSE](https://www.pse.com.ph/investing-at-pse/)).

Post-trade is a calendar, funding and counterparty problem. Settlement has been T+2 since 24 August 2023 with a **12:00 noon** deadline on the settlement date for cash and securities, and no T+1 plan has been found ([CN-2023-0040](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2023/08/CN_2023-0040-1.pdf#page=1); [SCCP Memo 06-0823](https://sccp.com.ph/resources/files/memos/2023/06-0823%20Announcement%20of%20SEC%20Approval%20of%20T+2-Related%20Amendments%20to%20the%20SCCP%20Rules%20and%20Operating%20Procedures.pdf#page=2)). The fail ladder is steep in time, not in fines: a fail costs PHP 1,000 plus 0.125% of its value if cured by 14:00 and PHP 1,000 plus 0.25% compounded daily after that, a cure is due by 09:15 on the next day, a buy-in or sell-out runs at 10:00, and the cash close-out price is the highest regular-lot price plus 10%, so a 5% buy-in on a PHP 25m fail costs about twenty times the later fine ([SCCP Memo 06-0823](https://sccp.com.ph/resources/files/memos/2023/06-0823%20Announcement%20of%20SEC%20Approval%20of%20T+2-Related%20Amendments%20to%20the%20SCCP%20Rules%20and%20Operating%20Procedures.pdf#page=4); [Chapter 10](../content/10-clearing-settlement.md)). SCCP is a limited-recourse central counterparty with no initial margin and a PHP 1,770.7m guaranty fund, its posted rulebook is the 13 March 2018 T+3 text, and the in-force rules must be rebuilt from memos ([PSE audited financial statements 2025](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/04/Annex-B-%E2%80%93-Audited-Consolidated-Financial-Statements.pdf#page=86); [SCCP Clearing House Rules, recourse cap](https://www.sccp.com.ph/resources/files/rules/SCCP_Revised_Rules_-_Approved_by_the_SEC_031318.pdf#page=27); [SCCP Memo 02-0125, early delivery](https://sccp.com.ph/SCCP/resources/files/memos/2025/02-0125%20Announcement%20of%20SEC%20Approval%20of%20Amendments%20to%20the%20SCCP%20Rules%20(Rules%203.4_%205.1.4_%206.2.8%20and%207.6).pdf#page=3); [SCCP Clearing House Rules, 2018 text](https://www.sccp.com.ph/resources/files/rules/SCCP_Revised_Rules_-_Approved_by_the_SEC_031318.pdf#page=1)). Foreign custodians' cut-offs fall earlier still (pre-matching at 09:00 and PHP funding at 14:30 on the day before, instructions by 09:35 on the day), on secondary evidence from a single custodian ([Clearstream settlement times](https://www.clearstream.com/clearstream-en/res-library/market-coverage/settlement-times-philippines-1279298)).

Foreign access is governed by room, not by the nominal limit. On PSE EDGE 218 of 281 companies, about 93.5% of the market capitalisation shown, carry a 40% foreign limit, 46 are at 100% and 15 at 0% ([PSE EDGE stock data](https://edge.pse.com.ph/companyPage/stockData.do)). Valid foreign buy orders are earmarked against the room when entered, so a resting bid consumes room before it fills, and a breaching trade must be unwound at the market price the same day or at the next open, with penalties under SRC section 54 ([Revised Trading Rules](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/04/Revised-Trading-Rules.pdf#page=17); [CN-2025-0035](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2025-0035.pdf#page=3)). Listed equities register through a registering bank with no BSP registration document, FX must be converted through an authorised bank, repatriation is full and immediate, FX-hedge notional may not exceed the exposure, and MSCI rates foreign-limit level, foreign room, FX liberalisation, stock lending and short selling "improvements needed" ([BSP FX Manual](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/MORFXT/MORFXT.pdf#page=46); [MSCI accessibility comparison](https://www.msci.com/downloads/web/msci-com/indexes/index-resources/market-classification/MSCI%202026%20GLOBAL%20MARKET%20ACCESSIBILITY%20COUNTRY%20COMPARISON%20REPORT.pdf#page=5); [Chapter 14](../content/14-foreign-access.md)).

Information leaks on a schedule. ITCH Total View is a full-depth market-by-order book with live indicative auction prices but no running statistics, resting orders carry no broker code, and executions and trades carry the passive and active broker identifiers unless anonymity is switched on, which PSE deferred on 3 October 2014 and nobody has found activated ([PSE ITCH specification](https://www.pse.com.ph/psetrade-xts/); [FIX/ITCH session, 3 Oct 2014](https://www.pse.com.ph/psetrade-xts/)). A patient limit order therefore signs its broker's name at every fill. Issuers must disclose material news within ten minutes, but the same-day release cut-off on PSE EDGE has been 4:00 pm since 25 May 2026, 45 minutes after the close, and EDGE itself failed on 19 January and 6 October 2026 ([CN-2026-0024](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/05/CN-No.-2026-0024.pdf#page=1); [CN-2026-0046](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/10/Access-to-Disclosures-via-PSE-Website.pdf#page=1)). Real-time data for automated trading needs a non-display licence of USD 7,500 a quarter per legal entity ([Non-Display Usage Policy](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/02/PSE_Non-Display_Usage_Policy_Guidelines_v1.0_June_1_2017.pdf#page=2); [Chapter 11](../content/11-market-data.md)).

## Eqlipse on 23 November turns every parameter into a version-gated bet

PSE's 9 July broker forum set the Nasdaq Eqlipse go-live ("NTE") for Monday 23 November 2026, a big-bang cut-over with no parallel run, after Saturday rehearsals on 31 October, 7 November and 14 November, and day-one support for limit orders only ([NTE broker forum](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/07/PSE-New-Trading-Engine-TradeX-Back-office_Broker-Forum_07092026-1.pdf#page=9); [NTE FAQ](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/08/Frequent-Asked-Questions.pdf#page=1)). Readiness was thin in July: of 40 participants with their own front end, one had completed connectivity testing and 18 were still analysing and developing to the FIX specification ([NTE broker forum](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/07/PSE-New-Trading-Engine-TradeX-Back-office_Broker-Forum_07092026-1.pdf#page=5)). The engine brings new FIX, ITCH and market-data specifications on separate sessions that are released only on request, so how thresholds, breakers, auctions and broker identifiers behave on the new engine is not public ([NTE FAQ](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2026/08/Frequent-Asked-Questions.pdf#page=3); [PSE new trading engine page](https://www.pse.com.ph/pse-new-trading-engine/); [Chapter 11](../content/11-market-data.md)). The rule package that was meant to accompany it (a lot of one share, a ten-band tick table, no odd-lot market, a run-off that matches at the closing price, and Negotiated Trades at ±5% of VWAP) was "For SEC Approval" or "Revising per Public Comments" on PSE's 17 August slide, and no approval or effectivity circular had been found by 6 October ([PSE 1H 2026 briefing](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/08/2026.08.17-PSE-STAR-1H-2026.pdf#page=9); [CN-2025-0046](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2025-0046.pdf#page=3); [CN-2026-0031](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2026-0031.pdf#page=3)). The search covered PSE's circular index, but the SEC, BIR and Official Gazette sites were unreachable, so an approval issued after PSE's last relay would not appear: "not found" is not "not granted" ([Chapter 17](../content/17-reform-timeline.md)).

PSE's record argues for caution about the date. T+2 held its date once the SEC had approved it, and the VWAP session launched on the day announced, but short selling took more than five years from SEC approval to go-live, and the GPDR, derivatives and 2023 board-lot targets all slipped ([CN-2023-0040](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2023/08/CN_2023-0040-1.pdf#page=1); [CN-2024-0012](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2024-0012.pdf#page=1); [CN-2018-0035](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2018-0035.pdf#page=1); [CN-2023-0056](https://documents.pse.com.ph/CircularOPSPDF/CN-2023-0056.pdf#page=1); [PSE 2026 President's Report](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/07/PSE-ASM-2026-PRESIDENT_S-REPORT.pdf#page=22)). The Eqlipse date was announced before the approvals it depends on, which makes it a target with execution risk, and PSE's materials do not say whether the engine would go live on the old lot and tick table or wait. The practical answer is three configurations behind date switches (XTS as in force, Eqlipse on the legacy tables, Eqlipse as approved), with each rule flipped on PSE's effectivity circular rather than on the engine date alone ([Chapter 16](../content/16-execution-implications.md)).

## Conclusion

The sharpest finding is how much of PSE execution risk is procedural rather than price. The tick table, the 14:48 cancel limit, the freeze, the noon settlement deadline, the ex-date purge and the foreign-room earmark are all published rules, yet each breaks an algorithm that treats the exchange as a generic continuous order book, and the largest pending change, a new engine with possibly new lot and tick tables, will arrive through circulars and a Day-1 limit-only cut-over rather than through a market event. The edge is therefore in the calendar and the circular: knowing which parameter flips on which document, holding a router that tolerates both regimes, and treating "no SEC approval found" as an open question to re-check before every rehearsal rather than a settled fact.

The public record is rich on rules and silent on quality. No document gives effective spreads, depth, intraday volume curves, the closing auction's share of volume, the per-order value limit or message-rate limits, and no study of tick size, price limits, the closing auction or broker-identifier transparency on the PSE was found ([Chapter 15](../content/15-empirical.md)). That silence is the practical conclusion: calibrate on your own ITCH capture, buy event-day history rather than a year, keep short and hedge logic gated off until a broker proves the plumbing, and re-verify the pending items (SEC decisions on the lot and tick package, the rehearsal results, the FTSE announcement of 6 October, the 16–18 November advisory and the MSCI November date) before trading size into the cut-over. The knowledge-base chapters linked above carry every parameter with its page-level source, and [Chapter 0](../content/00-at-a-glance.md) condenses them to one page.
