This chapter is synthesis. Every rule underneath it is sourced in the chapter it points to and nothing new is asserted about the rules here; where a conclusion is inference rather than rule text, it says so in words or in an *Inference* callout. It is written for the four parts of an execution stack (scheduler, order placer, risk layer, cost model) trading PSE equities on the rules in force on **6 October 2026**: the June 2010 Revised Trading Rules plus dated circulars, running on PSEtrade XTS.[^pse-revised-trading-rules:1][^pse-annual-report-2015:42]

Three facts frame everything. One exchange, one central counterparty and one depository mean there is no routing problem: every optimisation is scheduling, broker selection and counterparty management.[^pse-annual-report-2025:9] Algorithms live in the broker's system, not on a client's DMA line.[^pse-dma-rules:7] And the engine changes on **Monday 23 November 2026**: a big-bang cut-over to Nasdaq Eqlipse ("NTE") with no parallel run and limit orders only on Day 1, bundled with rule changes that had no SEC approval on record on 6 October. Every parameter below is therefore "in force, subject to change", and the last sections say what to switch on which PSE circular.[^pse-nte-broker-forum-2026-07-09:9][^pse-nte-faq-2026-08:1][^pse-analyst-briefing-1h-2026:9]

## The ten things that break a ported algorithm

A ported execution algorithm fails in these ten ways before it fails in any subtle one. Each item gives the rule, why it breaks a standard design, and the fix.

**1. Client-side algorithms cannot run on DMA, and there is nothing to co-locate.** "The DMA Facility shall not be used for High-Frequency and/or Algorithmic trading"; Sponsored Access is for qualified institutional buyers only, and the 2023 proposal to allow algorithms (manual parent, limit-order children) has no approval on record, though PSE says it has granted exemptions for child orders of conditioned parent orders.[^pse-dma-rules:7][^pse-cn-2023-0043:3] There is no co-location, no published message-rate limit and one FEOMS log-in per participant.[^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:31] The standard slicer on a client DMA line is unavailable. *Fix:* host the schedule in the broker's certified front end (human-entered parent, limit-order children), get exemption status, message-rate and per-order value limits in writing, and mirror the DMA pre-trade filters in your own risk layer ([Chapter 1](#/ch/market-architecture/dma-rules-and-algorithmic-trading), [Chapter 13](#/ch/regulatory-constraints/algorithmic-trading-hft-and-direct-market-access)).

**2. Market orders do not sweep, and the new engine opens limit-only.** A market order executes at the best bid or offer or the last traded price, whichever is better, and its remainder is "queued ... for immediate execution" (the approved handling of the remainder is not public); an unfilled market order at the open reserves the security; on Eqlipse Day 1 only limit orders exist.[^pse-revised-trading-rules:21-22][^pse-revised-trading-rules:33][^pse-nte-faq-2026-08:1] "Market" no longer guarantees immediacy. *Fix:* aggressive child orders are marketable limits priced inside the dynamic threshold and walked through the book; emulate stops and market-on-close in the router; price auction orders to guarantee participation ([Chapter 4](#/ch/order-types), [Chapter 6](#/ch/auctions)).

**3. A price breach freezes the stock for everyone; there is no volatility auction.** An order that would breach the static band (+50%/−30% of the reference price) or the dynamic threshold (20%, 15% or 10% of the last traded price) freezes the security: nobody can post, amend or cancel until Market Control acts, normally within five minutes. A dynamic breach is auto-accepted if the order is priced at the best bid or offer or is a cross, and auto-rejected in the five minutes before pre-close.[^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:26][^pse-cn-2020-0028:1-2][^pse-tpa-2026-0036-dynamic-threshold-review:1] An aggressive order locks your own cancels too, so a kill switch fails for up to five minutes. *Fix:* clip every price to the tighter corridor (read the collars from ITCH [k]), size sweeps so they cannot breach, send nothing breach-capable before pre-close and size passive orders to survive five minutes without a cancel ([Chapter 7](#/ch/price-controls)).

**4. The close is struck at 14:50, nothing cancels after 14:48, and 15:00–15:15 is not a market for ordinary orders.** The closing price is set at 14:50 by a call auction that opened at 14:45; the run-off trades only at that price; the Closing VWAP session takes VWAP-only trades of at least PHP 500,000 with one participant on both sides; the 12:00–13:00 recess allows no entry, amendment or cancellation.[^pse-approved-rules-vwap-trading-2024:6][^pse-approved-rules-vwap-trading-2024:7] An algorithm benchmarked to 15:15, or one that cancels into the last minutes, misses its price. *Fix:* commit closing interest before 14:48:00, treat 14:48–14:50 as irrevocable, use the run-off for residuals and do not plan on the VWAP session ([Chapter 3](#/ch/sessions), [Chapter 6](#/ch/auctions)).

**5. Tick and lot come from a 15-band table whose edges are cliffs, and a rewrite is pending.** Both are keyed on the day's reference price in a table unchanged since 26 Jul 2010; the relative tick jumps 2.5x at PHP 20, 500 and 5,000, so crossing a one-tick spread on PHP 10m costs 5.0 bp at a 19.98 reference and 12.5 bp at 20.00.[^pse-revised-trading-rules:21] One Lot One Share (lot = 1), a 10-band tick table and the end of the odd-lot market are "For SEC Approval" alongside the new engine.[^pse-analyst-briefing-1h-2026:9][^pse-cn-2025-0046-board-lot-trading-at-last:3-5] *Fix:* take lot and tick per security from a table keyed on the last adjusted close, hold both regimes behind a flag flipped on PSE's effectivity circular, and use prices valid under both readings near an edge ([Chapter 4](#/ch/order-types/board-lots-and-tick-sizes)).

**6. Resting orders are fragile, and nothing stops you trading with yourself.** Priority is price then time and is lost on a price change or size increase; there is no self-trade prevention, and a participant's counterpart order matches its own earlier order regardless of queue position; no cancel-on-disconnect is documented; and the Exchange cancels resting orders on dividend ex-dates, adjusted-close actions and lot changes.[^pse-revised-trading-rules:24-25][^pse-revised-trading-rules:31][^pse-fix-specification-v2-5:14] Cancel-replace to reduce size loses queue, multi-day orders vanish and no venue-side guard exists. *Fix:* amend down, add rather than upsize, build self-trade prevention across child orders, accounts and brokers, keep a router-side kill switch and re-arm multi-day orders daily and after every ex-date ([Chapter 4](#/ch/order-types/priority-and-amendments), [Chapter 5](#/ch/matching/no-self-trade-prevention-and-the-automatic-cross), [Chapter 10](#/ch/clearing-settlement/corporate-actions)).

**7. Headline volume is not volume you can trade.** Block sales (from PHP 20m) print outside the stock's own volume but inside headline turnover: 18.4% of 2026 value and 82.2% on 11 Sep 2026; regular ADV was PHP 6.25bn against a PHP 7.56bn headline; MSCI days traded a median 3.04x normal value; the PSEi closes at the day's high or low in about 38% of sessions (all computed).[^pse-dqr-2026-ytd-block-parse][^pse-monthly-report-2026-08:1][^pse-daily-quotation-report-series][^pse-weekly-reports-dataset] A headline-volume schedule overshoots by a fifth on average and several-fold on block days, and learns its curve from outliers. *Fix:* size off a trailing 20–60-day median of regular-market value, drop block days from calibration, run an event calendar and build the intraday curve from your own ITCH capture ([Chapter 5](#/ch/matching/block-sales), [Chapter 11](#/ch/market-data), [Chapter 15](#/ch/empirical)).

**8. Your fills carry your broker's code, and news can land after the close.** Resting orders are anonymous, but executions and trades carry the passive and active broker IDs unless anonymity is switched on; PSE deferred it in 2014 and no activation has been found.[^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:14-16][^pse-fix-itch-session-week01-2014-10-03:11] The EDGE same-day cut-off is 4:00 pm, 45 minutes after the 15:15 close.[^pse-cn-2026-0024-edge-cutoff-4pm:1] Patient limit orders leak identity at every fill, and a closing print can pre-date same-day news. *Fix:* treat broker choice as a disclosure decision, split parents across brokers, test the anonymity state from vendor samples and run a second news path ([Chapter 11](#/ch/market-data/broker-identifiers-and-information-leakage), [Chapter 13](#/ch/regulatory-constraints)).

**9. Foreign room is consumed by resting orders, and a breach is force-sold at market.** Valid foreign buy orders are earmarked against the room when entered (blocking other foreign buyers is a major violation), and a breaching trade is unwound at the market price the same day or at the next open; 218 of the 281 companies on PSE EDGE, about 93.5% of the market capitalisation shown, carry a 40% limit.[^pse-revised-trading-rules:17][^pse-cn-2025-0035-sec-declassification-mandate:3][^pse-edge-stock-data] A fill-time cap check is the standard design; here a resting bid consumes room and the cure has no price limit. *Fix:* gate foreign buys on live room (ITCH [f], decoded as signed) net of your own working orders, cancel unused bids before 09:15 and treat zero room as no bid ([Chapter 14](#/ch/foreign-access/behaviour-at-the-limit)).

**10. Shorting has never printed, and the hedge is offshore.** Short selling is live in 52 names (10% short-interest cap, uptick test, day orders, borrow before sale), yet every one of the 707 distinct daily reports from 6 Nov 2023 to 5 Oct 2026 shows zero volume; the onshore lending pool takes cash collateral only, the foreign-lender route awaits SEC approval and no derivative on PSE equities is listed.[^pse-short-selling-guidelines-2023-10:1][^pse-dssr-2026-10-05:1-2][^pse-market-reports-dssr][^pse-analyst-briefing-1h-2026:9][^pse-analyst-briefing-1h-2026:10] A long/short stack with a short branch assumes a product that exists in the rules but not in practice. *Fix:* gate the short branch off by default, plan weeks of lead time for borrow and run hedges offshore ([Chapter 9](#/ch/short-selling)).

## Designing the scheduler

The scheduler turns a parent order into time slices that respect hard windows, a calendar published late and a volume base smaller than the headline.

| PHT | State | Scheduler rule |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00–09:15 | Pre-open | Last chance to enter, amend or cancel auction interest (last second 09:14:59) |
| 09:15–09:30 | Pre-open no-cancel | Additions only; the indicative price is the only signal |
| 09:30:00 | Opening uncross | Deterministic at the scheduled second |
| 09:30–12:00, 13:00–14:45 | Continuous | Normal slicing |
| 12:00–13:00 | Recess | Hard stop: no entry, amendment or cancellation; Day orders stay |
| 14:45–14:48 | Pre-close | Last chance for closing interest (last second 14:47:59) |
| 14:48–14:50 | Pre-close no-cancel | Additions only, irrevocable |
| 14:50–15:00 | Run-off | Closing-price orders only; the price is already fixed |
| 15:00–15:15 | Closing VWAP | No ordinary orders; a separate broker facility (inference) |

Times are RTR Article II Section 2 as restated on 1 Feb 2024; the morning, afternoon and pre-close times have applied since 1 Mar 2022 and the VWAP session and 15:15 close since 1 Mar 2024.[^pse-approved-rules-vwap-trading-2024:3][^pse-approved-rules-vwap-trading-2024:6][^pse-cn-2022-0009-trading-schedule-mar-2022:1][^pse-cn-2024-0012-vwap-go-live:1]

- **Drive the state machine from the feed, not only a clock.** XTS-era ITCH publishes phase events and a Trading Schedule message with each event's scheduled time; a clock-only model breaks on the late opens of 9 Dec 2024 (09:55) and 24 Mar 2025 (11:10) and on the 3 Jan 2024 halt (09:32–11:56).[^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:10-11][^pse-cn-2024-0061-adjusted-schedule-2024-12-09:1][^pse-cn-2025-0015-adjusted-schedule-2025-03-24:1][^pse-cn-2024-0003-update-market-halt:1] The NTE specification is not public. Hold timestamps as configuration with an override channel: schedule circulars have given from one to 14 days' notice.[^pse-cn-2021-0059:1][^pse-cn-2020-0017:1]
- **The official close has had four regimes**: 15:30 until 13 Mar 2020, 13:00 in the shortened-hours periods of 2020–22, 15:00 from 6 Dec 2021 to 29 Feb 2024 and 15:15 since 1 Mar 2024. The last print is never the official close ([Chapter 17](#/ch/reform-timeline/version-gates-for-backtests-and-replay)).[^pse-memo-pre-close-schedule-2013:1][^pse-cn-2024-0012-vwap-go-live:1]
- **Auctions are deterministic, so reserve a share of the parent for them; how large is not in any public record** (no intraday profile, no auction share of volume). The close is not neutral (computed): the PSEi closed at the day's high on 21.3% and its low on 16.8% of 1,813 sessions; in the ten most liquid stocks the close is the high or low 13.2% and 14.7% of the time against about 2.7% each at random; on the last session of a month the top-100 figure is 43.8% against 34.0%.[^pse-weekly-reports-dataset][^pse-dqr-dataset]
- **Event days run at two to six times normal value.** MSCI implementation days: median 3.04x the prior-20-day median value (2.63x ex-block), 11 of 12 at 2x or more, net foreign selling on 10 of 12; FTSE third Fridays about 2.1x; PSEi event days 1.5–2.1x ex-block (computed). One deleted stock can be 18% to 38% of the market's value that day, and when MSCI's date is a PSE holiday the volume moves to the prior session (28 Aug 2026 for 31 Aug).[^pse-daily-quotation-report-series][^pse-cn-2026-0034b-non-trading-days-aug-2026:1][^pse-dqr-2026-08-28:12] The November 2026 MSCI date is unknown; the last two fell on 25 Nov 2024 and 24 Nov 2025, so it may land in the NTE go-live week.
- **Budget from regular-market ADV (about 81.5% of 2026 headline value).** Days with a block share above 30% (20 of 186) are outliers; blocks arrive on ITCH as [P]/[p] trades flagged B, so they can be separated live. A PHP 1bn parent is about 13% of average daily total value and 16% of regular ADV; about 14 names trade PHP 100m or more a day (computed).[^pse-dqr-2026-ytd-block-parse][^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:28][^pse-weekly-report-2026-10-02:1][^pse-dqr-dataset]
- **Calendar.** Closures arrive by circular 4 to 25 days ahead, emergency closures the evening before or same day; a PAGASA signal of 3 or more over NCR at 06:00 cancels the day and purges posted orders, so keep a "market not open" override. Settlement follows the SCCP, BSP and PCHC calendars, not PSE's (inference). 16–18 Nov 2026 is unresolved (CN-2026-0043 said "regular trading days" and CN-2026-0044 withdrew it the same day): model it as unknown ([Chapter 3](#/ch/sessions/trading-calendar)).[^pse-cn-2026-0027:1][^pse-cn-2025-0037:4-5][^pse-revised-trading-rules:35-36][^sccp-clearing-house-rules-2018:4][^pse-trading-day-advisory-2026-11-16-18:1]

<div class="callout infer">
<span class="label">Inference</span>

The close is struck 25 minutes before the market ends and, in the most liquid names, is the day's high or low about five times as often as chance. A schedule that back-loads a benchmarked mandate into the close concentrates price risk where the evidence is least favourable. Whether to back-load is a risk decision, not a liquidity one, and the in-sample facts (a short, falling sample) should be tested on your own data first.

## Designing the order placer

**Tick and lot.** The relative tick resets at each band floor and decays inside the band: 2.5x at PHP 20, 500 and 5,000; 2x at 0.50, 10, 100, 200, 1,000 and 2,000; 5x at 0.25.[^pse-revised-trading-rules:21] Improving a quote costs one tick, 25 bp at PHP 20.00 but 4 bp at PHP 499.80; in the PHP 20–50 band spreads are tick-constrained and queue position dominates (inference). Computed from end-of-day books, 33–44% of liquid-name spreads equal one tick, so price-time priority matters more than price improvement.[^pse-dqr-dataset]

- Lot rounding keys on the reference price: at 49.95 the lot is 100 shares, at 50.00 it is 10, so a 60-share residual is an odd-lot order one day and six lots the next; odd lots trade only in their own continuous-only book, so a residual has no auction path.[^pse-revised-trading-rules:21][^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:20]
- Whether the engine applies tick by reference price or order price near an edge is unstated; use a price valid under both (for 0.02 and 0.05 ticks, a multiple of 0.10).[^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:11-12]
- If One Lot One Share is approved, lot rounding stops binding and each broker's minimum order value does; the grid changes in the PHP 0.10, 0.50–0.995 and 10–20 bands (a PHP 15 stock moves from a 0.02 to a 0.05 tick, 13 bp to 33 bp) and the odd-lot book disappears.[^pse-cn-2025-0046-board-lot-trading-at-last:3-5]

| Action on a posted, unmatched order | Priority | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Reduce quantity | Kept | Amend down in place; never cancel-replace to reduce |
| Increase quantity | Lost | Send an additional order |
| Change limit price | Lost | Repricing is a new order at the back of the new level |
| Change trigger price | Lost | Stop logic may not exist on NTE Day 1 |
| Change validity | Kept | Extending a working order is free |
| Change client account code | Kept | Re-allocate between permitted codes without re-queuing |

Rules: RTR Article IV Section 13 and Implementing Guidelines XI.[^pse-revised-trading-rules:24-25][^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:18][^pse-fix-specification-v2-5:26] An iceberg must show at least 10% of its quantity in board-lot multiples; PSE publishes no replenishment-priority rule, so assume every tranche re-queues (inference).[^pse-revised-trading-rules:23] An amendment is a cancel/replace carrying the full requested state; ClOrdID must be unique across all of a firm's FIX connections within a day (the exchange may not check); the OrderID can change after an amendment, so track ClOrdID chains; and open multi-day orders are restated at the start of day, so expect a sequence gap at logon and recover with a ResendRequest.[^pse-fix-specification-v2-5:25-26][^pse-fix-specification-v2-5:9]

**Aggression and self-trades.** To take liquidity send marketable limits at or inside the best bid or offer: a limit at the touch is auto-accepted even beyond the dynamic threshold, one priced through the book beyond it freezes the stock.[^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:26] Build self-trade prevention yourself; the exchange will not stop you, and SRC §24.1(a) bars transactions with no change in beneficial ownership.[^ra-8799-src:24] The automatic cross is a broker-level internalisation priority, so expect lower passive fill probability at the touch where a large broker holds offsetting client flow (inference), and do not route offsetting fund flow through one participant without compliance sign-off.[^pse-revised-trading-rules:31]

**Hosting.** In practice institutional algorithms run in two ways (inference): broker-side in the participant's own certified front end with a human-entered parent and limit-only children (what the 2023 text would codify), or as buy-side parents sliced manually or with broker tools.[^pse-cn-2023-0043:6-9] Ask each broker in writing for its exemption status, message-rate limit, per-order value limit (the Exchange's cap is unpublished), kill-switch and mass-cancel procedure and NTE certification status: only 1 of 40 participants with their own front end had completed connectivity testing by 9 Jul 2026.[^pse-nte-broker-forum-2026-07-09:5] One FEOMS session per participant means shared capacity, and mass cancel exists only after a FEOMS or gateway failure.[^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:31][^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:18]

## Auction and close participation

**Replicate the uncross.** The auction price is a deterministic function of the visible book: maximum matched volume; then least unmatched; then market pressure (buy pressure takes the higher tied price); then the price nearest the reference; then the reference, which is the previous (or adjusted) close at the open and the last traded price at the close.[^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:13-16][^pse-revised-trading-rules:20] PSE's six worked examples are test vectors and [Chapter 6](#/ch/auctions/reference-implementation) reproduces all of them; example 3 (reference 900, tick 5; bids 915 x 40,000, 910 x 10,000; offers 905 x 40,000, 915 x 20,000) clears at **910** on market pressure.[^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:14] Build the indicative price from the ITCH [I] stream (Total View only) and compare it daily.[^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:17-18][^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:22-23] Validate in UAT what the rules leave open: allocation at the auction price, whether iceberg hidden quantity counts, the grid across a tick band and whether the NTE's logic is identical.[^pse-cn-2025-0046-board-lot-trading-at-last:5]

- With a limit-only Day 1 there is no market-on-open or market-on-close; price limits through the expected auction price to guarantee participation.
- The run-off is a fixed-price venue: zero tracking error against the official close, only fill uncertainty. On XTS an incoming closing-price order is rejected if a better-priced passive order rests, which after a genuine uncross happens essentially only when the pre-close did not cross (inference); the NTE draft matches it at the closing price.[^pse-revised-trading-rules:27][^pse-cn-2025-0046-board-lot-trading-at-last:5]
- Whether run-off orders can be amended or cancelled is contradicted (the Implementing Guidelines allow it, the ITCH state table does not): design for no cancel, and for no short orders.[^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:18][^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:10][^pse-short-selling-guidelines-2023-10:2] The closing price when the pre-close does not cross is not public; the working assumption is the last traded price (inference).

| Goal | Venue and window | What fixes the price | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Help set the official close | Pre-close call, 14:45–14:50 (cancel until 14:48) | Five-step rule on the whole book | Passive limit run over; no cancel after 14:48 |
| Trade at the official close | Run-off, 14:50–15:00, limit orders at the closing price | The price struck at 14:50 | Fill uncertainty; XTS rejection |
| Match the full-day VWAP | Closing VWAP, 15:00–15:15; at least PHP 500,000; one participant | Exchange VWAP excluding blocks, crosses, odd lots | Thin names; broker facility, not FIX (inference) |
| Pre-arranged size within ±5% of VWAP | Negotiated Trades (proposed), same window | Agreed price inside the band | Not in force |

Sources: [^pse-approved-rules-vwap-trading-2024:7][^pse-cn-2026-0031-negotiated-trades:3]. VWAP trades occurred on 67 of 186 days in 2026 and were 0.12% of value, so the session is not a liquidity pool.[^pse-dqr-2026-ytd-block-parse]

**Conduct.** Orders count whether or not they execute: the SEC's rules cover "all orders ... whether executed or not", CMIC's marking-the-close text reaches bids and offers "whether matched/executed or not", and brokers must weigh impact and timing and report suspicion within 24 hours.[^sec-2015-src-irr:68][^pse-cmic-rules:117][^pse-cmic-rules:116] Closing-auction participation is legitimate to achieve the close and problematic to set it, so keep an intent log for every child order, cancelled ones included. There is no named spoofing or layering rule, but those provisions would reach it (inference), and a VWAP built on a few shares in a thin name is cheap to influence.[^pse-eod-daily-quotation-2026-10-02:8]

## Price-control-aware risk checks

The risk layer must know which state a security, or the market, is in, because order handling differs.

| State | Trigger | Resting orders | Cancel allowed? | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Freeze | Order that would breach the static or dynamic threshold | Held | No, until Market Control acts (about 5 minutes) | Clip prices; at most three breaches per participant per day |
| Reservation | Market-on-open with no indicative price; unfilled market order at the open; indicative open outside the band; manual, before lifting a suspension | Kept | Yes, except crosses | Avoid market orders in thin names |
| Halt | Exchange, SEC or issuer request; up to one trading day | Kept | Yes, except crosses | Nothing matches; re-validate on resume |
| Suspension | Exchange, SEC or CMIC; MPO breach; late filings | Purged | Not applicable | Re-enter after resumption; first print unbounded if the band was lifted |
| Circuit breaker | PSEi −10/−15/−20%: 15/30/60-minute market halt, once per level per day | Not public | Not public | Design for both; no feed message |
| System-failure halt | Participants with over 50% of six-month ADTV (ex-block) cannot trade | Not public | Not public | Do not rely on the closing auction |
| Cancelled day | PAGASA signal 3 or higher over NCR at 06:00; President's cancellation | Purged | Not applicable | "Market not open" override |

Sources: [^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:26-28][^pse-revised-trading-rules:33-36][^pse-cn-2020-0044:2-4][^pse-cn-2025-0037:1-2][^pse-cn-2025-0037:4].

- **Thresholds.** Take the corridor from the feed's collars; refresh the dynamic-threshold clusters each February and August from the circular, which publishes them only as an image (read from it, clusters A, B and C held 62, 88 and 235 securities on 7 Aug 2026).[^pse-tpa-2026-0036-dynamic-threshold-review:1-2] The anchor is the last trade, so a deep passive limit can itself trip a freeze (inference). The static band, not the dynamic one, bounds the day: Asiabest fell 61.7% in four sessions on PHP 10–74m a day.[^pse-dqr-dataset]
- **Floors compound.** After n floor sessions the reference is 0.7^n of the start (70.0%, 49.0%, 34.3%, 24.0%, 16.8% for n = 1 to 5), so exit models for gap-down names must allow several sessions.[^pse-cn-2020-0028:1-2]
- **Halt and suspension screens.** Track the late-filing calendar (annual report day 105, quarterly day 45), the float cushion against the MPO tier, sustained limit moves with no disclosure and live tender offers; 65 of 385 securities with live frames were suspended on 6 Oct 2026.[^pse-listing-disclosure-rules:153][^pse-amended-mpo-rule-2026-08:6][^pse-sec-frames-snapshot] Infer a breaker from PSEi drawdown plus a burst of [H] state changes across constituents (inference); the circular's clock cut-offs were never restated for the 14:45 pre-close.[^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:14]
- **Foreign room.** Combine a static-limit table (40% for most names, 100% for 46 companies, 0% for 15) with the live [f] counter; a 0% name is untradeable for foreign accounts except through a PDR; a mis-flagged account code is likely to corrupt the count (inference).[^pse-edge-stock-data][^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:19-20]
- **Counterparty gates.** Check the clearing-member list (three of 125 members suspended on 6 Oct 2026) and track the capital proposals (PSE: PHP 50m by 2027 and PHP 100m by 2029; SEC draft: PHP 120m).[^sccp-web-membership][^pse-memo-tp-paid-up-capital-increase-2026-07:3][^pse-memo-2026-10-01-sec-rfc-src-28-1-33-1-capital:4]

## Information leakage

- **Broker codes.** Executions and trades name the passive and the active broker, so aggressor-broker footprints can be rebuilt from the tape without order-level attribution (inference).[^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:15-16][^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:19] A block removes the resting-order footprint, not the print, and the public Daily Quotation Report lists blocks without brokers; a block with a pre-set date is announced on PSE's website, and executing participants must name to PSE every client that traded the security between request and execution within five trading days.[^pse-eod-daily-quotation-2026-10-02:11][^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:25] Test the anonymity state by counting non-blank broker fields in a day of vendor samples, before and after the cut-over.
- **Disclosure timing.** Material news reaches PSE within ten minutes and before the media; the same-day cut-off is 4:00 pm and later filings post the next trading day, so they arrive with the 09:00 pre-open order window; an in-hours event brings a halt lifted one hour after dissemination, so there is no first-minute execution.[^pse-listing-disclosure-rules:139][^pse-cn-2026-0024-edge-cutoff-4pm:1][^pse-listing-disclosure-rules:140]
- **Second news path.** EDGE has no public API and failed on 19 Jan and 6 Oct 2026; run ITCH News, the Corporate Announcements Feed and the PSE website in parallel.[^pse-cn-2026-0046-edge-outage-access-to-disclosures:1]
- **Data cost.** Automated trading on real-time data needs a non-display licence of USD 7,500 a quarter per legal entity (USD 30,000 a year); historical order-book data costs PHP 10,000 a day, so buy event days, not a year.[^pse-non-display-usage-policy-2017:2-3][^pse-data-products-page]

## Cost model

With commission c in basis points, a round trip costs **RT = 2.24c + 14.12 bp**: 1.12c per side (commission plus 12% VAT), 0.56 bp PSE fee, 1.00 bp SCCP fee, 0.50 bp for the SEC "SRC fee" and 10 bp of stock transaction tax (STT) on the sale only. PHP 10m at 25 bp costs PHP 70,120 (70.12 bp) from 1 Jul 2025 and PHP 120,120 before.[^pse-investing-at-pse][^ra-12214-cmepa:16][^bir-rr-20-2025-stt:2] The SRC fee is secondary-only: show every figure with it (default on for institutional flow) and without (69.12 bp). Commission is the only negotiable term; there is no regulatory minimum since 18 Apr 2024 and no institutional rate card is public.[^pse-cn-2024-0029-min-commission-removal:1]

```python
from datetime import date

def explicit_cost_bp(side, commission_bp, trade_date, src_fee=True):
    cost = 1.12 * commission_bp + 0.56 + 1.00 + (0.50 if src_fee else 0.0)
    if side == "sell":                       # STT: seller-only, keyed on trade date
        cost += 10.0 if trade_date >= date(2025, 7, 1) else (60.0 if trade_date >= date(2018, 1, 1) else 50.0)
    return cost
```

- **STT is keyed on trade date and charged on gross value, not netted.** A backtest spanning 1 Jul 2025 with one rate misstates the sell side by 50 bp, and a book turning its capital 20 times a year pays about 2% in STT alone, so hurdles belong in round-trip basis points. A short sale pays STT on the opening sale and nothing on the cover ([Chapter 12](#/ch/costs/cost-parameters-to-version-by-trade-date)).[^ra-12214-cmepa:16][^pse-cn-2025-0028-cmepa-effectivity:1][^bir-rr-20-2025-stt:2]
- **Spread and impact are proxies (computed from end-of-day books).** Median quoted spread: 31 bp for turnover ranks 1–10, 35 bp for 11–30, 50 bp for 31–60, 87 bp for 61–100, 227 bp beyond; the Amihud-type figure is 0.56% of absolute move per PHP 100m in the top ten against 34% in ranks 61–100. After 1 Jul 2025 explicit costs dominate the spread in the top 30 names.[^pse-dqr-dataset]
- **Event-day impact.** Five of six heavy MSCI deletions closed 3% to 7% lower on the implementation day and five of the six rebounded 7% to 14% within five sessions; Ayala Land, which slid from the announcement, did not. Small Cap additions ran up 12% to 17% from the announcement and fell 6% to 9% within five days; CBC rose 38.9% on 89 times its median value before its PSEi inclusion and lost 6.1% over five sessions (computed). Size to the stock's own 20-day median value, not to the index.[^pse-daily-quotation-report-series][^pse-dqr-2025-01-31:1]

## Shorting and hedging reality

Treat onshore shorting as unproven plumbing and do not size alpha on short exposure in Philippine single names.[^pse-dssr-2026-10-05:1-2] If a broker enables it: send FIX Side = 5 (the Eqlipse specification is not public), in continuous trading only, as Day orders, unaggregated, never in the odd-lot market or a block, and check eligibility from ITCH [k] and the daily report (nine names are "B", buy-back only).[^pse-fix-specification-v2-5:53][^pse-short-selling-guidelines-2023-10:2][^pse-dssr-2026-10-05:1-2] The uptick or zero-plus test needs the last sale and last different sale in real time; the engine's entry-versus-match behaviour is unstated, so design for rejection at entry and re-validate resting short offers after every print.[^pse-short-selling-guidelines-2023-10:2]

Borrow is a pre-trade dependency measured in weeks: PSE pre-clears a master securities lending agreement in five working days (one for accession agreements), the BIR must register it within two weeks of execution in the Philippines or one month abroad, collateral is at least 102% (cash or government) or 105% (equity), the PDTC pool takes cash only, and a missed condition turns the loan into a taxable sale outside the exchange.[^pse-msla-clearance-guidelines-2026:2][^pse-msla-clearance-guidelines-2026:3][^sec-mc-7-2006-sbl-rules:10-11][^bir-rr-10-2006-sbl:9] Buy-backs go through the same participant and an undelivered short must be closed the business day after settlement.[^cmic-sbl-short-selling-guidelines:10][^sec-2015-src-irr:71] The foreign-lender route was filed with the SEC on 16 Apr 2026 and still awaited approval on 17 Aug.[^pse-cn-2026-0009:3][^pse-analyst-briefing-1h-2026:9]

Hedges are offshore. No derivative on PSE equities is listed (index futures are at request-for-information and funding stage, structured warrants are an SEC draft, GPDRs await approval), so index hedges use the US-listed EPHE (about US$132m, 40 holdings, ICT 22.7%), PLDT's ADR (TEL only) or OTC swaps. A dealer hedging a swap by selling PSE shares meets the uptick rule and the eligible list, so capacity can vanish in stress (inference); US hours do not overlap Manila's.[^pse-analyst-briefing-1h-2026:10][^pse-cn-2026-0018:2][^stockanalysis-ephe][^stockanalysis-phi] No single stock has a market maker: FMETF's obligation caps its spread (ten ticks, about 51 bp at PHP 97 on 2 Oct 2026; quotes of at least five board lots) and promises no depth, so treat every other displayed share as voluntary and size passive orders to the observed refill rate; two drafts are not in force ([Chapter 8](#/ch/market-making)).[^pse-etf-rules:22-23][^pse-eod-daily-quotation-2026-10-02:9][^pse-cn-2026-0026:4][^pse-cn-2026-0039-sec-market-making-rfc:13]

## Post-trade operations

Settlement is T+2 (since 24 Aug 2023) with a **12:00 noon** deadline on the settlement date (SD) for cash and securities. SD is the second SCCP business day after the trade date, where a business day excludes days on which PSE trading or BSP and PCHC clearing are cancelled; double-settlement days run 11:00 and 14:00 batches.[^pse-cn-2023-0040-t2-go-live:1-3][^sccp-clearing-house-rules-2018:4][^sccp-memo-07-0823-sec-approved-amendments:7-8] No T+1 plan was found, and BSP's 22/7 RTGS soft launch targeted for November 2026 does not by itself move SCCP's noon (inference).[^bsp-faqs-22x7-peso-rtgs-2026-07:1-4]

| When | Rule |
|---|---|
| 12:00–14:00 on SD | Late: PHP 1,000 + 0.125% of the fail |
| After 14:00 on SD | Fail: PHP 1,000 + 0.25% compounded daily |
| 09:15 on SD+1 | Cure deadline; preventive-suspension notice |
| 10:00 on SD+1 | Buy-in or sell-out as a normal PSE trade, no commission |
| If the buy-in fails | Cash close-out at the highest regular-lot price plus 10% |

- **The price move, not the fine, is the cost.** A PHP 25m fail (500,000 shares at PHP 50.00) costs PHP 32,250 if cured by 14:00 and PHP 63,500 after; a buy-in at +5% charges PHP 1.25m, about 20 times the later fine (arithmetic on the schedule). Cover every sale at the broker's PDTC account, or borrow, by 12:00 on SD ([Chapter 10](#/ch/clearing-settlement/fails-buy-ins-and-penalties)).
- **Ex-date is the last trading day before the record date** since 24 Aug 2023 (the January 2025 listing compilation still prints three days). For a record date of Thu 15 Oct 2026 the ex-date is Wed 14 Oct and the last cum-date Tue 13 Oct. The Exchange cancels resting orders on cash- and property-dividend ex-dates and closing-price adjustments with no carve-out for multi-day validity, so assume every resting order is purged; re-seed prices from the published adjusted close and cross-check it, because PSE once opened UBP unadjusted (21 Dec 2023).[^pse-cn-2023-0031-t2-settlement:1][^pse-listing-disclosure-rules:107][^pse-revised-trading-rules:25][^pse-cn-2023-0074-ubp-trading-suspension:1]
- **Custodian cut-offs fall before SCCP's noon (secondary, Clearstream only).** Pre-matching starts 09:00 on SD−1, PHP funding is due 14:30 on SD−1, valid instructions by 09:35 on SD, mismatches are re-instructed by 10:00, and there is no partial settlement or countervalue tolerance, so allocate at the close of trade date and avoid late partial fills with different counter-values. On the exchange side, unbundling and amendment deadlines are 14:00 on T+1 where the nationality flag changes and 17:00 where it does not (the SEC's rules say noon; confirm with the broker).[^clearstream-ph-settlement-services][^clearstream-ph-settlement-times][^pse-tpa-2011-0124-amended-implementing-guidelines:3-4]
- **Counterparty.** Exposure runs to the clearing member first and then to SCCP, a limited-recourse central counterparty with a PHP 1,770.7m guaranty fund and no initial margin; blocks sit outside the guarantee under the 2018 procedures and their settlement date is unresolved (the guidelines say T+0, the market is T+2), so treat them as bilateral credit exposure.[^sccp-clearing-house-rules-2018:27-28][^pse-audited-fs-2025:86][^sccp-clearing-house-operating-procedures-2018:23][^pse-implementing-guidelines-trading-rules:24] Brokers fail: EquitiWorld, suspended in October 2024, had its allocation plan approved 17 months later.[^pse-cn-2024-0053-equitiworld-involuntary-suspension:1][^pse-cn-2026-0012:2] The Daily Transactions Report used to reconcile with SCCP is to be retired with the new portal, so gate file ingestion on the cut-over.[^pse-nte-user-group-2026-01-15:19]

## Final caution

Rules change, and here the changes are scheduled. Treat every number in this knowledge base as configuration to be verified, not as a constant. The mechanisms (price-time priority, deterministic auctions, freeze rather than auction, broker-hosted algorithms, T+2 at noon) are stable; the parameters (lot and tick tables, thresholds and clusters, the close time, tax rates, eligible lists) are dated, and PSE's circulars, rulebooks and web pages disagree often enough that the dated circular is the only safe source ([Chapter 17](#/ch/reform-timeline/conflicts-and-stale-facts)).
