The Philippine Stock Exchange has one obligation-based liquidity regime and it covers one security. Part C of the PSE ETF Rules (SEC-approved 18 March 2013, published as Supplemental Rule 11) requires an ETF's market maker to repair a wide, one-sided or empty book, but only after the condition has lasted three minutes and then within ninety seconds, and only for the one ETF listed, FMETF.[^pse-etf-rules:1][^pse-etf-rules:22] Its market maker, First Metro Securities, is also its authorised participant.[^pse-etf-page] No single stock has a market maker; every other listed security relies on voluntary quoting.[^pse-press-2026-06-04-market-making]

Two things break assumptions carried over from venues with designated liquidity providers. First, the obligation is a ceiling on how bad FMETF's book may get, not a promise of a tight market: the maximum spread is ten ticks, the minimum quote is five board lots, and a wide book may persist for three minutes plus a ninety-second cure window. On 2 October 2026 FMETF closed at 97.00 bid / 97.50 ask, exactly the ten-tick ceiling.[^pse-eod-daily-quotation-2026-10-02:9] Second, the regime everyone is waiting for is not in force: PSE's amended Market Making Rules (consultation of 3 June 2026) and the SEC's umbrella Rules on Market Making (exposure draft of 13 August 2026) are both drafts, and PSE shows its own text as "Revising per Public Comments" on 17 August 2026.[^pse-cn-2026-0026:1-2][^pse-cn-2026-0039-sec-market-making-rfc:2][^pse-analyst-briefing-1h-2026:9]

## ETF market making rules in force

### Legal basis

The SEC's ETF Rules (SEC Memorandum Circular No. 10, series of 2012) define a market maker as "an Authorized Participant that assumes the obligation of providing two-way quotes following the rules of the Exchange and the Commission" (s.5(14)) and require at least two authorised participants, one of which acts as market maker (s.8(2)).[^sec-etf-rules:5][^sec-etf-rules:8] PSE's Part C implements those two sections, with Implementing Guidelines that carry the numbers.[^pse-etf-rules:15][^pse-etf-rules:21] The PSE ETF Rules also require each ETF to appoint at least two authorised participants (registered broker-dealers and active trading participants with paid-up capital of at least PHP 100m) and to designate at least one as ETF Market Maker.[^pse-etf-rules:3] An ETF is listed on a separate ETF Board.[^pse-etf-rules:3]

### Registration of an ETF market maker

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|

| Operating history | Continuously operating as a broker/dealer for the five years before the application[^pse-etf-rules:21] |
| Personnel | At least one Designated Specialist, a licensed salesman accredited by the Exchange; only accredited specialists may perform market-making functions[^pse-etf-rules:15][^pse-etf-rules:21] |
| Record | No violation of laws or serious non-compliance in the previous two years[^pse-etf-rules:21] |
| Application | Letter of intent, board resolution, processing fee of PHP 5,000 per issue (or such other amount as the Exchange prescribes)[^pse-etf-rules:21] |
| Checklist extras | The PSE application/renewal checklist adds SEC Form 28-BDA, two years of SEC good-standing and CMIC compliance certificates for the firm and the specialist, written Chinese-wall procedures under SRC Rule 34.1 and the original TP-ETF agreement; renewal needs one year of the same certificates[^pse-etf-market-maker-renewal-checklist:1][^pse-etf-market-maker-renewal-checklist:2] |
| Validity | Registration and specialist accreditation last one year and are renewable; both are non-transferable[^pse-etf-rules:15] |
| Precondition to quote | "Only when the ETF has a market making agreement with its Market Maker can its shares be subject of Market Making orders"; the maker must furnish its agreements to PSE's Listings Department[^pse-etf-rules:15][^pse-etf-rules:16] |

### Quoting obligations

The obligations "bite upon occurrence of Wide Spread"; they are not a continuous two-sided duty.[^pse-etf-rules:22]

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Maximum spread, price 0.0001 to 0.4950 | 20 ticks[^pse-etf-rules:22] |
| Maximum spread, price 0.5000 to 19.9800 | 15 ticks[^pse-etf-rules:22] |
| Maximum spread, price 20.0000 to 999.5000 | 10 ticks[^pse-etf-rules:22] |
| Maximum spread, price 1,000.0000 and above | 5 ticks[^pse-etf-rules:22] |
| Wide Spread means any of | (a) bid-ask spread beyond the limit above; (b) one-sided quotation; (c) no quotation on either side[^pse-etf-rules:16-17] |
| Trigger | The condition must occur continuously for at least **3 minutes** during Continuous Trading[^pse-etf-rules:17][^pse-etf-rules:22] |
| Cure | The maker must enter orders to eliminate the wide spread within **90 seconds** (must not exceed)[^pse-etf-rules:22] |
| Minimum order quantity | Each market-making order at least **5 board lots**; the Exchange may prescribe other volume requirements[^pse-etf-rules:22] |
| Daily presence | An active market-making order for at least **50%** of each trading day, measured as hours/minutes with an active order while the market is open[^pse-etf-rules:22-23] |
| Monthly presence | An active order for at least **80%** of the time over a month[^pse-etf-rules:23] |
| Parameter changes | The Exchange may revise the parameters for market conditions such as excessive volatility[^pse-etf-rules:17] |
| ETF-specific terms | The ETF's agreement with its maker may add obligations but may not supersede the Exchange minimum[^pse-etf-rules:25] |

The rule's own illustration is an ETF at PHP 25.00 with tick 0.05 and lot 100: ten ticks is PHP 0.50 (2.0% of price) and the minimum market-making order is 500 shares. Against a book of 24.90 bid / 25.50 ask (spread 0.60) that has persisted for three minutes, the maker may cure with a single sell of 500 at 25.40, a single buy of 700 at 25.00, or two-sided combinations; against a book with bids only (24.75 and 24.60) a single sell of 600 at 25.25 suffices.[^pse-etf-rules:23-24] A one-sided cure is enough as long as the resulting spread is within the limit.

Ticks and lots are the current table, shown in the CN-2025-0046 consultation paper as the "existing" table.[^pse-cn-2025-0046-board-lot-trading-at-last:4] The rule's own PHP 25 example (tick 0.05, lot 100) is consistent with it. Below PHP 0.50 the cap is 20 ticks, which is very wide in price terms (PHP 0.10, or 40% of price, at PHP 0.25, where the tick is 0.005). The percentage cap is a sawtooth: it jumps back up at every tick-band boundary, exactly as the [relative tick](#/ch/order-types/relative-tick-cost-at-band-edges-derived) does. The rule's illustration labels its price "LTP" (last traded price), whereas tick and lot are keyed on the reference price (the previous close);[^pse-etf-rules:23][^pse-revised-trading-rules:21] the text does not say which price selects the spread band, so on a day when a stock crosses a band boundary the two can disagree (gap).

<div class="callout infer">
<span class="label">Inference</span>

Three consequences follow from the arithmetic alone. (1) **Worst-case unobligated window.** A wide, one-sided or empty book must persist 3 minutes to count, and the maker then has up to 90 seconds, so such a book can lawfully last up to about 4.5 minutes. (2) **The monthly test is the binding presence test.** A maker that met only the daily 50% every day would average 50% and fail the 80% monthly test; the 50% figure only limits how bad one day can be. (3) **Presence is not quote quality.** Presence counts hours or minutes with an active market-making order; the text does not require that order to sit inside the maximum spread, and the maximum spread applies when a Wide Spread occurs. The text also defines Wide Spread only during Continuous Trading, so it creates no quoting duty in the opening or closing auctions ([Chapter 6](#/ch/auctions)). The denominator of "market is open" is not defined; if it means the continuous phases of the current schedule ([Chapter 3](#/ch/sessions)), 09:30 to 12:00 and 13:00 to 14:45 are 255 minutes, so 50% is about 128 minutes.
</div>

### Segregation, reporting, suspension and sanctions

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Accounts | Market-making orders only through the designated ETF market-making account; only a market-making account and an error account may be kept for that ETF; the maker is fully liable for all market-making orders and warrants their validity[^pse-etf-rules:17] |
| Specialist segregation | The Designated Specialist may not, on the same day, enter orders or handle client accounts or any other ETF; books and records must show who acted as specialist each day[^pse-etf-rules:18] |
| Reporting to PSE | Material changes, actual or expected breaches, disciplinary actions and litigation to be reported within one trading day of knowledge and no later than three trading days from occurrence[^pse-etf-rules:16] |
| Temporary suspension of duties | On the ETF's request: technical problems outside the maker's control, a specialist unavailable with no substitute, or other cases the Exchange deems necessary; the grant and its period are posted publicly[^pse-etf-rules:18] |
| Voluntary withdrawal | 30 trading days' notice plus the ETF's written consent; if consent is missing, a one-month cooling-off before re-registering; a sole maker's replacement must start no later than the withdrawal date[^pse-etf-rules:19] |
| Involuntary suspension or termination | Substantial non-compliance, unpaid dues, suspension by the SEC, CMIC or SCCP, or other reasons; re-registration only after three months[^pse-etf-rules:19][^pse-etf-rules:20] |
| Major violations | Using the market-making account for other trades, letting an unauthorised party use it, holding the ETF in the maker's proprietary account, reporting failures, continuous non-compliance; penalties under Article IX of the Trading Rules[^pse-etf-rules:20] |
| Monitoring | PSE "shall regularly monitor" performance against these parameters; a breach may lead to suspension or termination[^pse-etf-rules:25] |

The rule lists reporting failures as both a major violation and a minor one.[^pse-etf-rules:20][^pse-etf-rules:21] The issuer side is harsher than the maker side: the ETF pays PHP 500 per day for the absence of a market maker, trading in the ETF is suspended after one month without one, and a replacement must be engaged at least ten trading days before the old maker's termination takes effect or trading is suspended until one is.[^pse-etf-rules:13][^pse-etf-rules:11][^pse-etf-rules:10-11]

### Execution implications

- **Do not model FMETF as a continuous two-sided quote.** Model it as a book that may be empty, one-sided or at the ten-tick ceiling for up to about 4.5 minutes, with a market maker present at least 80% of the month.
- **Expect the quote to sit at the ceiling.** A maker acting at the minimum cures to exactly the limit, so a visible spread of ten ticks is compliant, not a signal of stress.
- **The minimum size is trivial.** At FMETF's P97 level the five-lot minimum is 50 shares, under PHP 5,000 (see the table above); resting size beyond that is not guaranteed.
- **Use iNAV as the pricing anchor for passive limits.** ETFs must disclose the indicative NAV every minute (or at another frequency the SEC or PSE sets), which gives an anchor for judging premium or discount when the book is wide.[^pse-etf-rules:7]
- **Watch the auctions separately.** The rule creates no obligation in the opening, closing or run-off phases; check the auction behaviour of the specific name ([Chapter 6](#/ch/auctions)).

## FMETF, the only obligated quote

PSE's ETF page lists First Metro Securities Brokerage Corp. as both the market maker and the authorised participant of FMETF and shows no other ETF.[^pse-etf-page] The 2013 rules require at least two authorised participants (with a PHP 100 per day penalty for a shortfall); the page shows one, and the pending ETF amendments would allow a single functioning authorised participant, which would then be deemed the ETF's market maker by operation of the rules (the suspension after one month without a market maker would stay). Whether the table is incomplete or the two-participant rule is unmet is not stated.[^pse-etf-rules:3][^pse-etf-rules:13][^pse-cn-2026-0029-etf-amend-consult:7][^pse-cn-2026-0029-etf-amend-consult:18] FMETF is also on the short-selling eligible list and on First Metro's own margin list ([Chapter 9](#/ch/short-selling)).[^pse-dssr-2026-10-05:1][^firstmetrosec-margin-trading-agreement-v072026:6]

FMETF's closing spread is exactly the ten-tick maximum for its price band. That is one closing snapshot, not a compliance test: the rule needs three continuous minutes in Continuous Trading, and the closing quote reflects the post-run-off book. No market-maker compliance, spread or presence statistics for FMETF are published in the PSE reports checked, and whether the maker has ever been sanctioned is not in the public record.

### Execution implications

- **Hedging with FMETF.** Plan around the rule maximum (ten ticks, about 51 bp at P97), not equity-like spreads; turnover on the snapshot day was only about PHP 5.6m, so size should be worked over time rather than posted.
- **Creation and redemption** run through authorised participants, and First Metro is the maker and the participant, so the maker can warehouse or hedge inventory through the basket (inference from the structure; the creation terms sit in the fund's registration statement).
- **Quote ownership.** Resting orders carry no broker code, so an FMETF quote cannot be attributed to First Metro from the book. If executions carry broker IDs (see below), First Metro as passive broker on an FMETF fill points to its market-making account only probabilistically, because the firm also trades for clients (inference).

## No equity market makers

PSE says its existing market-making rules are ETF-only, that it was "studying the introduction of market makers in the local market to boost liquidity" in July 2025 and again in March 2026, and that the amended rules may be "expanded later" to individual stocks and other exchange-traded products.[^pse-press-2026-06-04-market-making][^pse-asm-2025-presidents-report:30][^pse-analyst-briefing-3m-2026:14][^pse-analyst-briefing-1h-2026:9] PSE has a market-making collaboration agenda with Taiwan's exchanges (an MOU with Taipei Exchange of 10 June 2025 and learning sessions in December 2025), and one broker was planning an information session on market making for new products such as GPDRs and ETFs.[^pse-analyst-briefing-3m-2026:15][^pse-annual-report-2025:29][^pse-analyst-briefing-3m-2026:14]

The older trading rules contain only vestigial concepts. The Revised Trading Rules (2010 base text) define a market maker as a trading participant "primarily engaged by the issuer to provide liquidity to a Security, pursuant to an agreement and in accordance with the requirements of the Exchange" and define the Designated Specialist; Section 22 bars a Designated Specialist from handling client accounts (while letting the proprietary trader also handle a market-maker account); and Article V, "Market Making", is an empty heading marked "still pending final approval by the SEC".[^pse-revised-trading-rules:9][^pse-revised-trading-rules:28][^pse-revised-trading-rules:30]

Two adjacent facts matter if equity market making is introduced:

- **Short-sale relief.** SRC Rule 24.2-2.5 already exempts "a sale due to a bona fide market-making or arbitrage activity executed by a broker dealer authorized to engage in such activities" from the uptick requirement "unless otherwise provided by the Commission".[^sec-2015-src-irr:71] PSE's draft would add "exemption from short selling restrictions" as a negotiable incentive.[^pse-cn-2026-0026:5]
- **Tax.** The 0.1% stock transaction tax applies to "every sale ... other than the sale by a dealer in securities", whose gains are ordinary income instead (RA 12214, in force since 1 July 2025); whether a market maker qualifies as a dealer depends on facts ([Chapter 12](#/ch/costs)).[^ra-12214-cmepa:16][^bir-rr-20-2025-stt:2-3]

### Execution implications

- **No quoting counterparty exists for any single stock.** Every unit of displayed depth is voluntary: broker proprietary desks and other participants who can withdraw. Quote persistence and depth recovery must be measured, not assumed ([Chapter 15](#/ch/empirical)).
- **A future equity maker would quote from borrowed stock.** The SEC draft lets makers rely on securities-borrowing arrangements, so borrow availability becomes a hard dependency ([Chapter 9](#/ch/short-selling)).

## Are market-maker quotes identifiable in the book?

Not in the public feed specification. The ITCH Add Order message carries order number, side, quantity, orderbook and price and no broker or market-maker field.[^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:14] Executions are sent either anonymously ([E], [C], [P]) or with passive and active broker identifiers ([e], [c], [p]), "based on whether the market is implementing Broker Anonymity ... as announced by the exchange".[^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:15-16][^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:25] The orderbook-restrictions message carries a Short Sell Eligible flag but no market-maker marker.[^pse-itch-equities-feed-spec-v2-3:12] PSE can identify market-making orders internally because they must use a segregated account.[^pse-etf-rules:17][^pse-cn-2026-0026:8]

| Qualification (Rule III) | Trading participant; unimpaired paid-up capital under an SEC risk-based framework and **never less than PHP 150m** (the Exchange may calibrate upward); track record or key-personnel experience; clean two-year record; Designated Specialist with at least five years' experience; the Exchange accredits (no separate SEC process) but must notify the SEC within three business days and report quarterly[^pse-cn-2026-0039-sec-market-making-rfc:5][^pse-cn-2026-0039-sec-market-making-rfc:6] |
| Designation (Rule IV) | The Exchange designates eligible securities by liquidity, float and trading activity under SEC-approved criteria; **several market makers per security allowed**, with coordination left to Exchange rules[^pse-cn-2026-0039-sec-market-making-rfc:6] |
| Equity obligations (Rule VII) | Firm two-sided quotes for an Exchange-set minimum share of the session; maximum spread (in price terms) and minimum quote size set by the Exchange, "risk-sensitive and differentiated by liquidity profile, public float and trading activity"; quotes firm except for system failure, market-wide disruption or force majeure; inventory or "securities borrowing arrangements" suffice ("not required to hold inventory at all times"), and reliance on borrowing requires SBL-rule compliance[^pse-cn-2026-0039-sec-market-making-rfc:8] |
| Permitted and prohibited acts (Rule VI) | Own-account trading, bona fide hedging, block trades and stabilisation permitted; wash trades, artificial prices, misuse of non-public information and collusion with issuers prohibited[^pse-cn-2026-0039-sec-market-making-rfc:7] |
| Incentives (Rule IX) | The Exchange "shall grant" incentives such as reduced transaction fees, **liquidity rebates**, simplified onboarding and enhanced trading facilities, rewarding quoting performance and guarding against excessive cancellations, artificial trading and excess inventory; **subject to prior SEC approval**, periodic review against performance data and public disclosure of their general nature[^pse-cn-2026-0039-sec-market-making-rfc:9] |
| Transparency (Rule X) | The Exchange publishes maker names and securities, designated securities, obligations and performance standards, start/end/suspension of arrangements and issuer relationships, and may publish aggregate indicators (average quoted spread, quote presence, traded volume, depth); quarterly reports to the SEC; maker records kept at least five years[^pse-cn-2026-0039-sec-market-making-rfc:10] |
| Surveillance and suspension (Rule XIII) | Monitoring of quoting, inventory, spoofing, layering, wash trades and marking the close; temporary suspension for system failure, force majeure, extraordinary volatility, halts, or "lack of inventory despite reasonable efforts, unavailability of securities lending, settlement disruption, or corporate action/record date restrictions"; sanctions are suspension or revocation, fines and referral[^pse-cn-2026-0039-sec-market-making-rfc:12][^pse-cn-2026-0039-sec-market-making-rfc:13] |

<div class="callout infer">
<span class="label">Inference</span>

The PSE draft's PHP 100m capital test is below the SEC draft's PHP 150m floor, so one text will have to change, which is consistent with PSE "revising" after the SEC exposure. PSE's separate ETF proposal that the maker "need not be" an authorised participant conflicts with the SEC ETF Rules definition (a market maker is an authorised participant), so it needs a matching SEC amendment before it can take effect.[^pse-cn-2026-0029-etf-amend-consult:4][^sec-etf-rules:5] The draft 80%-monthly-presence omission would, if carried into the final ETF annex, loosen the one presence test that actually binds today. Neither draft states quote size in shares; both inherit "board lots", which the engine change makes ambiguous.
</div>

## What the absence of obligated liquidity means for passive execution

<div class="callout">
<span class="label">Consequence</span>

For any PSE single stock there is no designated quoter to absorb size at the touch in stress, no quoting-obligation floor under depth, and no maker-taker economics shaped by makers. Passive orders compete only with voluntary liquidity. For FMETF there is a floor, but a loose one: ten ticks, five lots, a 4.5-minute grace and 80% monthly presence.
</div>

### Execution implications

- **Equities.** Treat displayed depth as unreserved: size passive child orders to the observed refill rate, not to the posted book, and assume liquidity withdraws with volatility ([Chapter 7](#/ch/price-controls), [Chapter 15](#/ch/empirical)).
- **FMETF.** Expect gaps and ten-tick quotes at the open and after fast moves; price to iNAV; do not rely on size beyond five lots.
- **Feed handling.** Do not assume market-maker flow can be filtered from public data; no tag exists in the legacy ITCH specification.
- **Watch list.** (i) Final SEC Rules on Market Making (effective 15 days after publication); (ii) PSE implementing guidelines naming Designated Securities (the likely first candidates are PSEi names, ETFs and GPDRs, which is inference) with spreads, sizes and presence by tier; (iii) fee-concession and rebate schedules, which need SEC approval and will change passive-fill economics around makers' quotes ([Chapter 12](#/ch/costs)); (iv) the Eqlipse feed's anonymity setting.

## What is not in the public record

- The final text and effective date of the PSE amended rules and the SEC Rules on Market Making; PSE's status after 17 August 2026.
- Any incentive economics (fee-concession amounts, rebate rates) and any FMETF quoting, spread or presence statistics; whether FMETF's maker has ever been sanctioned.
- The market-making agreement between FMETF and First Metro, which may impose obligations beyond the Exchange minimum.
- The denominator of the presence test, whether the three-minute clock runs across the midday recess, and whether the last traded price or the reference price selects the spread band.
- Whether the PSE ETF page's single authorised participant for FMETF means the two-participant rule is unmet or the table is incomplete.
- Broker-anonymity status in production feeds and whether the Eqlipse feed flags market-making orders.
- Private liquidity-provider or price-support arrangements for individual stocks outside the ETF rules.
